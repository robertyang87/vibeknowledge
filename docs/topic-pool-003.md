## Candidate pool (one axis weak — recorded, not scheduled)
- **Airplane food tastes bland** — universality W: cabin taste suppression (salt and sweet down by roughly a third in Fraunhofer's Lufthansa-commissioned tests) is solid B-grade backing, but frequent flyers are not most people.
- **The stamp card that starts with two stamps** — universality M/W: the endowed-progress experiment is A-grade (Nunes and Dreze 2006; completion 34% vs 19%), but the physical stamp-card moment is narrow; digital progress bars dilute the principle.
- **A thousand-person poll predicts a nation** — universality M/W: sampling-error mathematics is S-grade and the 1936 Literary Digest failure (2.4 million responses, wrong) against Gallup is the canonical story, but the audience witnesses polls rather than living a moment in them.

## Out as pitched
- **Hot water freezing faster (the Mpemba effect)** — backing W for a standard episode: conditionally real, mechanism unsettled after half a century of argument. Admissible only as a clearly labelled open-question special.
- **The hospital that wins every department and loses overall (Simpson's paradox)** — universality W as pitched: comparing hospitals by department success rates is a fabricated consumer moment. The principle stays in the library; it needs a genuinely lived scene before it may return.
