# Topic Pool 001: First Candidates, Scored Against the Topic Gate

Written 2026-10-29, following the topic gate in
`docs/baseline-workflow.md` (step 1). Hook lines below are English
working versions; an episode's actual copy is written in the
video's language and stored as copy when the episode starts.

Gate key: **E** = everyday experience, **Y** = works as a "you"
question, **J** = a journey the camera can follow, **S** = hard
numbers are sourceable, **F** = a library style fits natively.
All five must pass.

## Batch 1: recommended first five

### 1. The blue dot: how your phone knows where you are

- Working hook: "You open the map. The dot knows where you stand.
  It is listening to clocks in the sky, and a one-second timing
  error would put you 300,000 km away."
- Journey: timing signals fall from satellites about 20,200 km
  up, reach the phone in roughly 0.07 seconds (the same headline
  number as the fiber reference, by a different road), and the
  phone turns four arrival-time differences into one point.
- Payoff turn: the satellite clocks run fast by about 38
  microseconds a day unless Einstein's corrections are applied;
  without them the dot drifts by kilometers daily.
- Style: Satellite Briefing.
- Gate: E yes, Y yes, J yes, S yes, F yes.
- Why first: it reuses the proven shape of the reference hit
  (one number, one journey, one idea) on a different topic, and
  it is the strongest style fit in the library.
- Risk: keep the relativity beat to one sentence; it is a payoff,
  not the lecture.

### 2. The broken QR code that still scans

- Working hook: "You scan a code with a corner torn off. It still
  opens. The code was designed to be partly destroyed."
- Journey: follow one scan into the grid: finder patterns, data
  modules, and the error-correction copies that rebuild the
  missing corner (up to about 30 percent damage at the highest
  correction level).
- Style: Data Storytelling or Pictogram Motion.
- Gate: E yes, Y yes, J yes, S yes, F yes.
- Note: the easiest production in this pool (grids are native to
  code drawing) and the most save-and-share friendly.

### 3. Noise-cancelling headphones fight noise with noise

- Working hook: "Your earbuds erase the engine roar by making a
  second sound: the exact opposite of the noise."
- Journey: ride one sound wave in, watch the microphone catch
  it, the chip flip it, and the two waves flatten each other
  before they reach the eardrum.
- Style: Sci-fi Hologram HUD or Data Storytelling.
- Gate: E yes, Y yes, J yes, S yes, F yes.
- Note: wave cancellation is the cleanest physics diagram in the
  pool; strong comment potential (owners arguing about brands).

### 4. Your phone counts steps with a spring smaller than a hair

- Working hook: "You walk 10,000 steps. A tiny spring inside
  your phone felt every single one of them bounce."
- Journey: zoom from the phone in a pocket down into the MEMS
  accelerometer: a proof mass on microscopic springs, its motion
  turned into the step count.
- Style: Microscope Story.
- Gate: E yes, Y yes, J yes, S yes, F yes.
- Note: plays to the project owner's professional home ground
  (sensors and embedded systems), where our fact cards can be
  better than generalist creators'.

### 5. Your deleted photo is not deleted

- Working hook: "You delete a photo. For a while, it is still
  all there. Only its name is gone."
- Journey: into a flash memory cell: trapped electrons hold the
  photo, deletion only erases the index entry, and the data
  survives until something overwrites the space.
- Style: Data Physicalization or Exploded Teardown.
- Gate: E yes, Y yes, J yes, S yes, F yes.
- Note: the most argued-about topic in the pool; handle the
  privacy angle carefully and fact-check the modern phone case
  (encryption and trim change the recovery story by platform).

## Batch 2: strong reserves

- **The 30-year wait for blue light.** Why every lamp and screen
  needed one missing color; the blue LED story ends at a Nobel
  Prize. Fits the creator's best-category list directly.
- **Your recorded voice is not your voice.** Bone conduction
  explains why everyone dislikes their own recordings; the phone
  is honest, your skull is the filter. Psychology plus acoustics.
- **The phone that vibrates when it did not.** Phantom vibration:
  a psychology episode, cheapest to produce, good variety slot.
- **Fast charging that slows itself down.** A lithium battery's
  last 20 percent is filled slowly on purpose; the journey is
  into the cell, with heat as the antagonist.
- **Walking through a doorway and forgetting.** The doorway
  effect: memory treats a door as an event boundary. Psychology,
  very relatable, diagram-light.
- **Wi-Fi through one wall.** Why one wall can halve your speed
  and why 5 GHz dies faster than 2.4 GHz; journey follows one
  packet and one wavefront through the flat.

## Rejected for now, with reasons

- The fiber photo journey itself: it is the reference creator's
  flagship; our version would read as a copy, and the topic gate
  is for finding our own.
- CPU and chip-architecture internals: fails the everyday gate.
  A pipeline diagram is a lecture, not a phenomenon. (Sensors
  pass where architecture fails because the body feels them:
  steps, face unlock, the blue dot.)
- Anything needing real footage of people or products: this
  format is code-made schematic film; if a topic cannot be drawn,
  it is not a topic for this channel yet.

## Suggested order

1. Blue dot (GPS) as episode 1: the format proof.

3. Noise cancelling as episode 3: the comment-driver.
Then re-score the pool from the 24 hour and 7 day logs of the

## Prior-work check on Douyin (2026-10-29)

Method: Douyin web search, video tab, four to six query variants
per topic, top results reviewed. Douyin web search shows likes,
not views; all numbers below are likes at check time. Verdict key:
(a) a hit already owns the topic and angle, (b) exists but weak
or differently angled, (c) basically untouched. **No topic in
Batch 1 scored (a), and none scored (c): all five scored (b).**

The pattern behind the pattern: the vibe knowledge contest
produced a gold rush in the second half of October 2026. Every
obvious phone topic was attempted by many creators within the
same two weeks, in the same style, and almost none of them broke
out. The topic gate now has a sixth question: has the contest
crowd already strip-mined this topic in the last month?

### Per-topic verdicts

- **GPS blue dot (b, downgraded).** The reference creator
  himself published this exact topic about six days before the
  check (3 minutes 24 seconds, about 2,073 likes), well below
  his fiber hit, and at least five other vibe entries on phone
  positioning appeared the same week. The fragments are also
  taken separately: four-satellite explainers and 38-microsecond
  explainers exist as small videos. The strongest positioning
  videos overall are older talking-head lectures (47k and 156k
  likes), not vibe animations. Do not open the channel with the
  same full-line script; the topic stays in the pool only with a
  different spine (for example: the satellite does not know you
  exist; the phone only listens).
- **QR code (b, most crowded).** More than twenty near-identical
  titles in about a week, almost all under 300 likes; the best
  exact-match older video has about 2,726 likes, and the only
  five-figure relative is a 13-minute full-principles lecture
  (about 30k likes). A generic "missing corner still scans"
  script is the single most strip-mined angle in this pool.
  Viable only as a visible experiment: the four correction
  levels side by side, or the correction that 30 percent is
  about position, not area. Two small videos already touch the
  position angle, so even that must be executed better, not
  merely repeated.
- **Noise cancelling (b).** A dozen vibe attempts in two weeks,
  all under about 1,000 likes; older 3D explainers top out near
  10k likes, and the best related video (about 20k likes) is
  about the stuffed, dizzy feeling, not the wave journey. The
  topic is validated but its vibe-form ceiling so far is low;
  the wave-cancellation journey alone is not a differentiator.
- **Step counting / MEMS (b, collision risk).** One vibe video
  from 2026-10-18 already runs almost exactly our script (a
  2 mm accelerometer, a silicon proof mass on springs, comb
  capacitance), at about 1,322 likes; a second vibe entry sits
  at 5 likes. Doing the same script would read as a remake.
  Re-angle or drop: the honest remaining spines are how the
  phone tells steps from shakes, and why step counts can be
  cheated.
- **Deleted photos (b, upgraded to first).** The shallowest
  crowd of the five. Vibe attempts exist but at 3 to 16 likes;
  the mechanism is usually told at hard-disk level, and
  flash-cell-level short explainers are close to absent.
  Adjacent demand is proven at scale: privacy-warning and
  storage-cleanup neighbours hold 105k and 31k likes. Our
  differentiation: go to the flash cell, then land on the
  practical question the neighbours raise but never answer with
  mechanism: what actually erases a photo, and what to do
  before selling an old phone.

### Revised order

1. **Deleted photos**, re-angled as above, as episode 1.
2. GPS and QR return only with a new spine, after a fresh
   prior-work check on that spine.
3. MEMS and noise cancelling are parked unless a new spine
   appears.
4. The Batch 2 reserves have not been prior-work checked yet;
   check them before promoting any of them, because the same
   contest flood may have reached them too.

Lesson recorded: in a contest window, a topic's novelty decays
in days. The prior-work check is now a standing step between the
topic gate and the fact card in our process; add it to
`docs/baseline-workflow.md` on the next revision of that file.
