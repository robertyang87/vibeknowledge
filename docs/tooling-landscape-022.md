## LottieFiles/motion-design-skill - the principles layer

2,777 stars, 197 forks, MIT. Created 2026-03-12, last
pushed 2026-05-18, repository about 34 KB: a principles
document, not a codebase. LottieFiles' own skill:
universal motion design principles for AI agents - timing,
easing, choreography, and the Disney animation principles
adapted for UI and agent-made films.

Why it is filed: it is the only principles-layer entry in
this index. Every other filing is a style, a tool or a
pipeline; our own animation standards were built
empirically, audit by audit. This is the canonical sheet
behind that class of rules. Role: reference shelf. Read it
when a motion question is about principle rather than
style - reading it is the adoption; at 34 KB there is
nothing to install. Its quiet since May 2026 is expected:
principles do not churn.

## alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion - the explainer sibling

6,226 stars, 623 forks, MIT. Created 2026-10-06, last
pushed 2026-10-16, repository about 78.6 MB. The sibling
of huashu-motion (landscape 017) by the same author: that
skill makes product launch films; this one is art
animation - 35 art styles and 9 explainer grammars, in the
repository's own description "make pictures move with
code". The source post names whiteboard, Vox-style and
3Blue1Brown-style explainer registers among its coverage.

Role: reference shelf, not installed. The borrowable part
is the grammar inventory - nine named ways to structure an
explainer is a checklist our Treatments can be compared
against - not the styles themselves, which belong to other
creators' films. Quiet for about three weeks at filing;
watch item.

## feitangyuan/onetake - one continuous camera, no licence

3,728 stars, 257 forks, NO LICENCE (GitHub reports
NOASSERTION). Created 2026-09-26, last pushed 2026-10-21,
repository about 128.6 MB. Motion films that never cut:
one continuous camera, every beat grows out of the one
before, spring physics and cursor-driven change; aimed at
product launches and feature demos.

Filed at the owner's direction with the licence warning
standing: with no licence, the repository may be read for
its aesthetic and nothing - code, assets, prompts - may be
reused in any production. What is noted for the shelf is
the register only: continuity between beats as a
transition idea.
