## What the film is
A dark-background line-and-data-graphics biography of Zheng Qinwen, built
entirely from drawn elements: wireframe court, scoreboards, line charts,
maps, text cards. No real match footage appears in any sampled frame.
Fixed furniture throughout: chapter head top-left ("01 - <name>"), creator
brand top-right, bottom-centre bilingual captions - large white Chinese,
small English beneath. Keyword colour semantics: lime-green for positive
anchors (final, title, qualifying), red for negative numbers (the ranking
low, set points faced).

## Copy mechanics worth stealing
- One portable metaphor in the title - "a road built shot by shot" -
  and a closing aphorism that restates it. The film never quantifies the
  metaphor (see Gaps).
- Every beat is carried by a drawn artefact that grows, stamps or crosses
  out in sync with its caption: charts fall, maps draw their route, a
  house gets its SOLD stamp, set points get crossed out as SAVED. The
  picture does the explaining; the caption only names it.
- Numbers inventory shown: years 2002/2014/2015/2022/2025.07/2026;
  ages under-13 and 14-15; scores 7-6 5-4 (final, as drawn), the Swiatek
  three-set board, 6-2 6-3 (Olympic final), 4-5 (first set, final);
  rankings top 5, No. 160; three set points; two months to comeback.
  No prize-money figure appears in any sampled frame.

## Gaps - where a better film wins
1. The title promises "shot by shot" and the film never counts a single
   shot. The metaphor is asserted, not demonstrated. A version that keeps
   a running, verified count - matches, sets, games, or estimated strokes
   across the comeback run - turns the title into the data spine.
2. The story stops at the title. The documented sequel (our own records:
   post-title withdrawals from four straight events with a right-shoulder
   problem, season over, rehabilitation in Barcelona, ranking No. 22 on
   2026-10-19) is the honest ending and the emotional point: the road is
   still being built. Every load-bearing number also needs an independent
   fact card before reuse - the No. 160 low, the final's date and the
   opponent's seeding are the film's claims, not verified facts.
3. Flat wireframe minimalism throughout: no depth, no light, no venue.
   Our 3D miniature craft (Ep1) is a clear visual upgrade on the same
   drawn-film discipline.
4. The best drama is buried: three set points saved in the final sits at
   1:38. A cold open on that moment, then the rewind, beats result-first
   alone.
5. No narration could be verified from the viewing setup; the film reads
   as caption-carried. Our line scores and designs sound deliberately.

## Disposition
Study object only. The borrowings are furniture and mechanics (chapter
heads, colour semantics for numbers, graphics that grow in sync with
captions, one metaphor carried from title to closing line). Our episode,
if commissioned, is an original production under the baseline workflow:
fact card first, the shot-count spine as its demonstration, the complete
arc including the post-title season end, 3D craft.
