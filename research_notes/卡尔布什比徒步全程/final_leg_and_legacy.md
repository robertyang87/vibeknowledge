# Karl Bushby's Goliath Expedition: Final Leg (2024 to Nov 2026), Totals and Legacy

Status as of 2026-11-05: **COMPLETED.** Bushby reached his mother's home in Hull on **Monday 2 November 2026**, 28 years and one day after setting out from Punta Arenas, Chile, on 1 November 1998 — [ABC News/AP](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/karl-bushby-completes-28-year-31000-mile-walk-137765943); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/british-adventurer-karl-bushby-returns-home-after-28-year-walk-around-world)

## Timeline of 2024–2026 stages (dates and places)

### Takeaway
After swimming the Caspian Sea in 2024, Bushby crossed the Bosphorus into Europe on 2 May 2025. He then walked Turkey, Bulgaria, Romania, Hungary, Slovakia/Austria, Germany, Belgium and France. Visa limits (Turkish 90-day visas, then Schengen's 90/180 rule) broke the walk into stints, with waits in Mexico in between. He landed in England on 5 Oct 2026 and reached Hull on 2 Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Aug 2024:** He began swimming the Caspian Sea from Kazakhstan toward Azerbaijan to avoid Iran and Russia, with Angela Maxwell and two Azerbaijani swimmers. Wikipedia gives 288 km (179 mi) over 31 days and 132 hours of swimming — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby). Other sources differ on the duration: 27 days ([ExplorersWeb, Jun 2026](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-refused-entry-into-channel-tunnel/)) and 32 days at sea ([ExplorersWeb, Nov 2026](https://explorersweb.com/?p=119070)). **Conflicting figures.**
- Iran was off the route because he could not get an Iranian visa after Turkmenistan. He backtracked to Uzbekistan and crossed the Kyzylkum Desert before the Caspian swim — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-refused-entry-into-channel-tunnel/)
- **Spring 2025:** He reached Turkey. **2 May 2025:** he crossed the Bosphorus Bridge in Istanbul into Europe, his first time back in Europe since he started. His 90-day Turkish visa was running out, so he left the same day and waited about three months in Mexico. **About 19 Aug 2025:** he flew back to Istanbul and resumed. At that point he had walked about 47,000 km, with about 3,000 km left and a target of September 2026 — [ExplorersWeb, 20 Aug 2025](https://explorersweb.com/?p=107610)
- **Sep 2025:** He finished Turkey and entered Bulgaria and the Schengen Area. **Oct 2025:** Romania, 2,213 km (1,375 mi) from the UK. **15 Nov 2025:** Budapest. **Dec 2025:** Slovakia. The Schengen 90-in-180-day limit forced him to leave the zone — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- **Jan 2026:** He waited in Mexico for his visa allowance to reset. About two months later he returned to Hungary and continued — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- The route ran Hungary → Austria → Germany → Belgium → France. During each 90-day limit he used Mexico, Canada or the US as a base — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/?p=119070)
- **May 2026:** He finished Germany and entered Belgium, according to [ExplorersWeb, 19 Jun 2026](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-refused-entry-into-channel-tunnel/). Wikipedia says "early June" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby). **Minor conflict.**
- **Mid-Jun 2026:** His visa expired and he went back to Mexico — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- **Sep 2026:** He resumed walking in Belgium, reached Tournai on 18 Sep and entered France on 20 Sep — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- **5 Oct 2026:** He landed at Shakespeare Beach, just south of Dover, after swimming from France. Wikipedia notes this was exactly 28 years after he left Hull — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby); [Oman Observer/AFP](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1197524/features/lifestyle/brit-adventurer-returns-to-uk-after-28-year-walk); [ITV Calendar, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.itv.com/news/calendar/2026-10-06/man-who-set-out-to-walk-globe-arrived-back-in-uk-28-years-later)
- **28 Oct 2026:** He was seen walking near Market Rasen, Lincolnshire — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/british-adventurer-karl-bushby-returns-home-after-28-year-walk-around-world)
- **1 Nov 2026:** He crossed the Humber Bridge in thick mist, the day before he arrived in the city centre — [Kuwait Times/AFP](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/52869/lifestyle/bushby-ends-epic-28-year-trek-across-jungles-and-frozen-sea/)
- **2 Nov 2026:** Civic welcome at Hull City Hall, then the final walk to his mother's house — [Culture Hull](https://www.culturehull.com/blog/post/hull-to-welcome-home-karl-bushby-as-28-year-journey-comes-to-an-end/); [ABC/AP](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/karl-bushby-completes-28-year-31000-mile-walk-137765943)

### Inferences
- In 2025–26 the European leg was paced by visa-day budgets rather than by how fast he could walk. He walked in roughly 90-day blocks with gaps of two to three months in between.
- The Dover→Hull leg took about four weeks (5 Oct to 2 Nov) and passed through Lincolnshire (Market Rasen), then over the Humber Bridge from the south bank.

### Gaps

## How the English Channel was crossed, and under what rules

### Takeaway
Eurotunnel (Getlink) refused to let him walk the service tunnel in June 2026. After long negotiation, French and UK authorities gave him special permission to swim from the French coast, which French rules normally forbid. He swam in stages over three days, about 20 hours in the water, sleeping on a safety vessel each night and restarting each morning from his last-known position. He landed on 5 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- His core rule was no motorised transport (and no boats or sailing), plus no going home until the walk was finished — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-refused-entry-into-channel-tunnel/); [Kuwait Times/AFP](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/52869/lifestyle/bushby-ends-epic-28-year-trek-across-jungles-and-frozen-sea/)
- **Jun 2026 (ExplorersWeb, 19 Jun):** Eurotunnel refused his request to walk the service tunnel. It called the tunnel a critical safety and maintenance route and said "we are unable to accommodate Mr Bushby's wishes". It added that closing it for 15 hours, or over two days, would pose safety risks and disrupt maintenance. Bushby replied: "if I have to swim across the channel, then I am prepared to do that." — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-refused-entry-into-channel-tunnel/)
- A change.org petition urged Getlink to allow the walk — [change.org](https://www.change.org/p/allow-karl-bushby-to-walk-through-the-channel-tunnel). Euro Weekly News (7 Aug 2026) framed the refusal as "jobsworths" threatening the final stretch — [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2026/08/07/karl-bushbys-28-year-walk-home-to-hull-hits-channel-tunnel-block-jobsworths-threaten-final-stretch/)
- French rules forbid Channel swims that start from the French coast. In September he learned his expected exemption had not been granted, then got approval after negotiation — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/?p=119070). Wikipedia says that in early September he announced the French government had given special permission, with a planned date of 1 Oct — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby). **Sequencing differs slightly between sources.**
- He got permission from both the UK and French authorities. His team did not publicise the swim, citing safety and security — [Oman Observer/AFP](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1197524/features/lifestyle/brit-adventurer-returns-to-uk-after-28-year-walk)
- Swim method: three days, about 20 hours in the water, sleeping on a safety vessel and restarting each morning from the last-known position — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/?p=119070). Wikipedia says "about four days of swimming and resting on support boats" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby). **Conflict: 3 vs about 4 days.**
- On the beach to meet him were his father, his brothers, a small group of friends, and Werner Herzog with a film crew — [Oman Observer/AFP](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1197524/features/lifestyle/brit-adventurer-returns-to-uk-after-28-year-walk); [ABC/AP](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/karl-bushby-completes-28-year-31000-mile-walk-137765943)
- His quote on landing: "feels good to have UK soil under my feet after all these years… It hasn't been easy, and that's part of the point." — [Oman Observer/AFP](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1197524/features/lifestyle/brit-adventurer-returns-to-uk-after-28-year-walk)

### Inferences
- This was a staged swim, not a continuous one under Channel Swimming Association rules, so it would not count as a CSA-ratified Channel swim. It mirrors his staged Caspian method of 2024.
- In Aug 2025 ExplorersWeb wrote that "swimming and sailing are excluded" ([link](https://explorersweb.com/?p=107610)). That appears to be a reporting error, or a reference to an earlier preference: he had already swum the Caspian, and the actual rule only bans transport.

### Gaps
- I could not find the exact French departure point (for example, Cap Gris-Nez) or the dates the swim started.
- Wording of the Eurotunnel refusal comes via ExplorersWeb citing the BBC; I could not fetch the BBC original.

On 2 Nov 2026 hundreds of people, up to about 1,000 by AFP's count, welcomed him in Queen Victoria Square and at City Hall. The Lord Mayor, Cllr Maria Coward, greeted him on stage, and a private reception followed. He then walked on to his mother Angela's terraced house, which had a "Welcome Home Son" banner. Totals are given as about 50,000 km / 31,000 mi, 4 continents, 30 countries, 28 years and more than 10,000 days. The original plan of 36,000 mi (58,000 km) is still quoted by some outlets.

## Controversies and criticism

### Takeaway
The main criticism is that the walk was not continuous. Only about 13 of the 28 years were spent actually walking, and he flew out (mostly to a base in Mexico) during visa, COVID, funding and bureaucratic gaps. Each time, he resumed from the exact spot where he had stopped. Bushby says he never broke his rule of covering every yard under his own power.

### Cited Findings
- Of 27 years, an estimated 13 were spent actively walking. The rest went to travel restrictions, bureaucracy and detours — [Stars and Stripes, 6 Dec 2025](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/europe/2025-12-06/british-army-veteran-20001754.html) (via search snippet)
- His rules allowed him to fly away from a dead end, as long as he resumed exactly where he had stopped. He "never broke his one simple rule – to travel every yard under his own steam" — [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/europe/2025-12-06/british-army-veteran-20001754.html) (search snippet)
- During pauses he lived at a base in Mexico and resumed from the exact point — [Kuwait Times/AFP](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/52869/lifestyle/bushby-ends-epic-28-year-trek-across-jungles-and-frozen-sea/)
- Some question whether the journey fits the definition of "walking around the world", given the flights and the self-set rules — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/?p=119070). Reddit commenters argue the "unbroken" framing is misleading — [Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1pfdkfs/snapshots/2025-12-06T02%3A59%3A07.44517Z) (opinion)
- Long delays included: the 2008 financial crash, which cost him sponsors and halted him from 2008 to 2010; the Russian visa saga, including a five-year ban in March 2013 that was later overturned, with permission granted in 2014; a protest walk from LA to the Russian Embassy in Washington DC in 2012; COVID in 2020; China (Xinjiang, 2018), where he continued under police escort; 18 days in jail in Panama in 2001; and arrest in Russia in 2006 after the Bering Strait crossing — [ABC/AP](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/karl-bushby-completes-28-year-31000-mile-walk-137765943); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby); [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/?p=107610)
- The route is not a true circumnavigation: it runs from southern Chile to Hull and does not cross the Atlantic. This follows from the route descriptions — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/british-adventurer-karl-bushby-returns-home-after-28-year-walk-around-world)

### Inferences
- The Channel swim was staged and needed government exemptions. Purists may also question it, but I found no published criticism of it specifically.

### Gaps
- I found no mainstream (BBC/Guardian/Times) critical piece. The criticism found is mostly online commentary plus the AP/Stripes framing.

## Post-expedition: reflections, book, film, health, family, plans

### Takeaway
Werner Herzog is making a feature documentary, *Walk of Life*. His production company has backed the trek for 16 years. A sequel to his 2005 memoir *Giant Steps* is due, and six talks are booked over two months (including Hull on 13 Nov 2026). Bushby is living with his mother, is reconnecting with his son Adam (36) and his 12-year-old granddaughter, is considering retraining as a teacher, and speaks openly of a post-expedition "black hole".

### Cited Findings
- **Film:** Werner Herzog's feature-length documentary *Walk of Life*. Herzog's production company has supported the trek for 16 years with money, logistics, filming equipment and archiving.
