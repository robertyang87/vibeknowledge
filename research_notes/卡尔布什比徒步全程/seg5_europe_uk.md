## 1. Georgia (Nov 2024 – early Feb 2025)

**Takeaway:** Georgia is very thinly documented. The anchors are that he left Baku on about 10 Nov 2024, was in Batumi on 30 Jan 2025, and crossed at Sarp on 5 Feb 2025.

- He left Baku on 10 Nov 2024 (karlbushby.world, via search summary) or 9 Nov (Baku photo, onthegosolo). His own site says that from Baku to Istanbul was "more than 2,000 km" in about 5 months, across Azerbaijan, Georgia and Turkey.
- On 30 Jan 2025 he posted a Facebook video from Batumi. He had spent "a long month" walking busy highways in rain and icy conditions — [onthegosolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/).
- **Gaps:** No source names his Azerbaijan–Georgia border post (Red Bridge vs Lagodekhi), and none confirms Tbilisi.
  - **[INF]** The likely route was Baku → Ganja → Red Bridge → Tbilisi → E60/S1 west via Gori and Kutaisi → Batumi, i.e. the main highway that the "busy highways" remark fits. This is unverified.
  - Daily Sabah (5 Sep 2025) says he "entered Türkiye through Georgia in February".
- **Distance:** The AA put the remainder at 6,200 km to the UK when he entered Turkey (Feb 2025).

## 2. Turkey (5 Feb – 2 May 2025; ~20 Aug – 1 Sep 2025)

**Takeaway:** Turkey took two visa-limited stints.
- **Stint 1** ran from Sarp along the Black Sea to Samsun, then inland via Bolu to Üsküdar, then over the Bosphorus on 2 May. He flew to Mexico the same day.
- **Stint 2** ran from Istanbul to Edirne/Kapıkule in about 12 days (~20 Aug – 1 Sep).

**Stint 1**
- **Entry:** Sarp Border Gate on 5 Feb 2025. He began in Kemalpaşa (Artvin) and walked about 20 km to Hopa on day 1. Age 55 at the time; he said he had been walking 9,584 days. Quotes: "Finding a sleeping spot on the route in Türkiye is quite difficult since there are cliffs and the sea"; "If the roads are good, I am happy." — [Daily Sabah/AA](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/british-adventurer-enters-turkiye-on-foot-during-world-journey/news); [Daily Sabah on X](https://x.com/DailySabah/status/1887977197492707393)
- **Route in his own words:** "from the coast of Georgia down through Türkiye, and then along the coast all the way to Samsun, and then you come inland and then all the way here to Istanbul." — [Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/british-adventurer-arrives-in-istanbul-after-26-year-walk-208670)
  - **[INF]** The coast road (D010) would pass Rize, Trabzon, Giresun and Ordu. None of these is attested in sources found.
- **Samsun:** about 17 Mar 2025, roughly 460 mi from Istanbul — onthegosolo (citing Türkiye Gazette).
- **Bolu:** 16 Apr 2025, after 295 mi (474 km) in the past month; 160 mi (260 km) to Istanbul — onthegosolo.
- **Istanbul:** Arrived in Üsküdar. karlbushby.world says 25 Apr; the AA story ran 30 Apr. He was awaiting permission either to walk the bridge (closed to pedestrians since the 1980s, per [The Hull Story, Mar 2025](https://www.thehullstory.com/allarticles/karl-bushby-long-walk-home)) or to swim the Bosphorus. Permission came about a week after arrival (site summary). He gave about 3,000 km as the distance left to France. Quote: "The people have been fantastic."
- **2 May 2025:** He walked the 15 July Martyrs Bridge. Being back in Europe for the first time since 1998, he "raced to the airport" because the 90-day visa was expiring → Mexico — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-back-in-europe-begins-last-leg-of-journey/)

**Stint 2**
- He resumed from Istanbul on about 19–21 Aug 2025 (ExplorersWeb says ~19 Aug; FB reels said ~21 Aug).
- He finished at Edirne on 1 Sep 2025 and exited via the Kapıkule Border Gate.
- Quotes: "Türkiye was amazing, a magnificent country… I lost a lot of weight in those mountains… People stop to talk to you in the street." — [Daily Sabah/AA, 5 Sep 2025](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/british-traveler-praises-turkiyes-hospitality-after-completing-walk/news)

**Conflicts and weak points**
- **[CONFLICT/WEAK]** celebritytalk.co.uk says "June 2025 Armenia – waiting for a Turkish visa". Every other source says Mexico, so treat Armenia as an error.
- **[CONFLICT]** Some summaries put Wikipedia's Asian-Turkey leg at "31 Mar–2 May 2025". This is inconsistent with the 5 Feb Sarp entry.
- **[CONFLICT]** Media gave his age as 55 (Feb) and 56 (Apr/Sep).
- No Türkiye Today article was found. The Turkish coverage comes from AA via Daily Sabah and Hürriyet Daily News.

## 3. Bulgaria (1 Sep – ~late Sep/early Oct 2025)

**Takeaway:** He entered via Kapıkule (Svilengrad side), was pushed off the Maritsa motorway by police, went through Plovdiv to Sofia (mid-Sep, fan meet-up), then over the Stara Planina to Vidin and across the Danube into Romania. Bulgarian media's headline story is that **Bulgaria was the only country to fine him in 28 years**.

**Route**
- Bulgaria was the "first fully European country" he had entered since 1998.
- Route given by Webcafe.bg (18 Sep 2025): Road 8 via Haskovo [sic], Plovdiv and Pazardzhik; Road 82 via Samokov and the Iskar reservoir; Sofia (staying several days); then Vidin over Stara Planina and the Danube bridge into Romania — [Webcafe.bg](https://webcafe.bg/gradat/karl-bushbi-obikoli-sveta-pesha-v-balgariya.html)
- **[INF]** "Haskovo" probably means passing near or through Haskovo Province (Harmanli/Lyubimets).

**Police stop and fine**
- RFE/RL (23 Sep 2025, citing a 360° magazine post of 4 Sep): a police patrol stopped him on the Maritsa motorway between Lyubimets and Harmanli. His homemade two-wheeled cart, "The Beast", is not allowed on motorways, so he was sent to second-class roads. Quote: "Motorways are the safest places to walk." — [RFE/RL](https://bulgaria.rferl.org/a/karl-bushby-koito-obikalia-okolo-sveta-ot-27-godini-chovek-na-denia/33522528.html)
- Nov 2026 Bulgarian coverage, quoting documentary-maker **James Grant**, who accompanied him for part of the journey: he was fined for walking on the motorway hard shoulder with the cart, and "no other country had fined him." The amount is not given — [24chasa](https://www.24chasa.bg/ozhivlenie/article/23869418); [Fakti.bg](https://fakti.bg/life/1097032-za-28-godini-pateshestvenikat-karl-bushbi-e-globen-samo-v-balgaria)
- **[INF]** James Grant is probably part of the documentary crew. His affiliation is unverified.

**Sofia**
- He arrived on about 16 Sep 2025 and held a meet-and-greet on 20 Sep at 20:00 at Tipsy Bar & Café — onthegosolo; Webcafe.bg. bTV interviewed him ("In Russia it was very hard to convince them I wasn't a spy") — [bTV](https://btvnovinite.bg/predavania/tazi-sabota-i-nedelia/britanec-obikalja-sveta-pesha-v-rusija-beshe-mnogo-trudno-da-gi-ubedja-che-ne-sam-shpionin.html)
- **[CONFLICT]** 24chasa dates the Sofia fan meeting to "November 2025", which is impossible because he was in Hungary then. September is correct.

## 5. Hungary, "Slovakia", Austria (Nov 2025 – Apr 2026)

**Takeaway:** He reached Budapest on 15 Nov 2025, followed the Danube northwest past Komárom, and stopped at Győr just before Christmas. After the Mexico break he resumed near the Hungary–Austria border in March 2026. He then walked the Danube through Vienna/Tulln and Linz, where Angela Maxwell joined in early April. The **Slovakia** claim is doubtful.

**Hungary**
- Budapest, 15 Nov 2025. He planned to head for Vienna — Wikipedia (TikTok).
- Győr just before Christmas 2025, "a few days' walk from the Austrian border" — BBC Jan 2026 via [VisasNews](https://visasnews.com/en/karl-bushbys-28-year-walk-around-the-world-tested-by-visas-and-borders/)

**Slovakia [CONFLICT]**
- Wikipedia says "In December 2025 he was reported to be in Slovakia", citing Noviny.sk (5 Dec 2025). But Noviny's own text says only that the last public record was from "Komárom" (the Hungarian spelling) and that he is "maybe already near Bratislava" — [Noviny.sk](https://www.noviny.sk/zahranicie/1142597-chodi-uz-takmer-30-rokov-karl-bushby-sa-blizi-k-cielu-svojej-cesty-peso-okolo-sveta)

Whether he later walked Győr → Bratislava → Vienna in March 2026 (which would put Slovakia on the route) is **unresolved**. **Austria**

- 24 Mar 2026: Danube footpath near Vienna, just past Tulln (video posted 29 Mar) — onthegosolo.

**Mexico break #2:** Dec 2025 → Mar 2026. In Jan 2026 he told the BBC: "If I had it my way, I would keep going. But the rules mean that I have to visa bounce" and "It's going to be a hard stop; the end of a life." He chose Mexico for its low cost, its welcome, and its closeness to US supporters — VisasNews. ## 7. Belgium (≈ early Jun 2026; resumed ~early/mid-Sep – 20 Sep 2026)

- Early June: he entered Belgium, "well under 1000 kilometers" from the finish. In mid-June the Schengen limit was reached → Mexico break #3. Eurotunnel's refusal came on about 17 Jun.
- He resumed in September (Euro Weekly News, 7 Aug, had said he planned to return in early September).
- **Havré → Mons:** BBC's Kevin Shoesmith joined him for a 5-mile stretch to Mons Grand-Place, the Belfry and 30°C heat, after he had already walked 10 mi before lunch.
  - Quotes: "Tenacity is emperor"; "These towns can be fun assault courses"; "I had no confidence… I was an underachiever"; "It's the relationships. I've fallen in love twice, lost them, and that leaves marks."
  - Shoesmith described the leg as "part of the final march to Calais".
  - Source: [headtopics mirror of the BBC piece, 19 Sep 2026](https://uk.headtopics.com/news/i-joined-karl-bushby-on-his-round-the-world-walk-87908730)
- **Tournai, 18 Sep:** the NoTélé interview (published 19 Sep). Tournai was not on the planned route; he was diverted there for administrative procedures and stayed a few hours. He gave about 600 km to Hull. Quotes: "C'est effrayant. C'est un peu intimidant"; "Ma raison de vivre, mon but dans la vie, est sur le point de s'arrêter brutalement." — [NoTélé](https://www.notele.be/si103-media167756-apres-28-ans-de-marche-autour-du-monde-karl-bushby-fait-escale-a-tournai-avant-le-dernier-defi.html); follow-up [NoTélé on the homecoming](https://www.notele.be/si103-media168855-passe-par-tournai-en-septembre-karl-bushby-retrouve-enfin-sa-famille-apres-28-ans-de-marche-autour-du-monde.html)

- **[INF]** He probably resumed in Belgium near where he stopped in June. Mons/Havré sits in Hainaut, which suggests a route entering via Liège/Namur/Charleroi, but that is not attested.

## 8. France (20 Sep – ~3 Oct 2026) and the Channel swim (~3–5 Oct 2026)

- He entered France on 20 Sep (Wikipedia, citing NoTélé). The Calais coast was his goal (BBC). No French towns are named in sources, and no French media found covered his transit.
- **[INF]** The likely route was Tournai → Lille region → Calais/Cap Gris-Nez, about 120–150 km and roughly a week, then waiting for a weather and tide window.
- **Permit saga:**
  - Early Sep: he announced French permission and a target of 1 Oct.
  - Late Sep: "We thought we got permission to swim from the coast of France. Turned out that is not the case" (AFP); he was in a "semi-panicked state".
  - The permit was then secured with French authorities and the coastguard.
  - Sources: [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-is-back-in-the-uk-after-swimming-the-channel/); [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/travel/article/rjt11alcjmx)
- **The swim:**
  - 3 days, about 20 h in the water, wetsuit (Ynet adds flotation gear). Nights on a boat, restarting each day from the last position.
  - "There was no training at all"; "I swim like a cat thrown into a bathtub."
  - Departure point and time were kept private for safety (Yorkshire Post, via Wikipedia).
  - He landed at about 15:30 BST on Mon 5 Oct at Shakespeare Beach, met by his father Keith, his brothers, friends and **Werner Herzog with a film crew**.
  - "It feels good to have UK soil under my feet after all these years" (ITV).
  - 5 Oct was exactly 28 years after he left Hull for Chile.

## 9. England: Dover → Hull (5 Oct – 2 Nov 2026)

**Takeaway:** About 400 km / 250 mi in about 24 walking days (9 Oct – 2 Nov). Only Dover (departed 9 Oct), Lincolnshire / Market Rasen (28 Oct), the Humber Bridge (1 Nov) and Hessle → Hull (2 Nov) are attested. **No source found names any town in Kent beyond Dover, nor London, Essex, Cambridgeshire or Peterborough.** Searches for Canterbury, Maidstone, Medway, Dartford/Thames crossings, Cambridge, Peterborough, Stamford, Lincoln and Brigg returned nothing about him.
