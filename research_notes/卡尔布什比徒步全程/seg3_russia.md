## 1. Chukotka, 2006–2008

### 2006: landfall and stalemate
(Full detail is in verify_bering_russia.md.)
- The two men landed near Uelen on about 31 March 2006, having missed Provideniya in a blizzard and ice drift. They were detained on about 1 April by border officer Captain Sergei Kim.
- In Lavrentiya, Father Leonid hosted them.
- On 13 April, NTV reported a court hearing in Lavrentiya. The charge had been reduced from illegal border crossing to violating border-crossing rules.
- The fine was 2,000 RUB each, and an expulsion order was issued.
- The Chukotka okrug court annulled the expulsion. Their lawyer was Alexander Perelygin. The ruling is reported around 5 May.
- Source: https://bpclub.ru/topic/5638-... (forum thread compiling NTV/RIA reports)
- Vokrug Sveta notes Bushby carried a **rifle** on the strait in case of polar bears. KP says it was a **revolver**. https://www.kp.ru/daily/277829.3/5323802/

### 2007: restart, with snowmobile transfer only
- **Tia-ostrova, 20 Mar 2007:**
  - Kieffer and Bushby returned to Russia on 19 March.
  - Their papers got "особое внимание" because of the previous year's incident.
  - Plan: «Из Анадыря они доберутся до Уэлена на снегоходах» (from Anadyr they would reach Uelen by snowmobile). From Uelen they would walk via Egvekinot to Anadyr, then via Markovo to Magadan, finishing in early summer.
  - https://tia-ostrova.ru/news/obschestvo/38080/
  - Note: this snowmobile ride was a **transfer back to the stopping point, not progress along the route**.
- **dailynews.co.za, 14 Mar 2007:**
  - Bushby was to fly Nome → Anadyr and resume from where border guards first stopped him.
  - His father said they got the Chukotka permits only "a week or two ago".
  - https://dailynews.co.za/news/eish/2007-03-14-daredevil-resumes-world-trek-in-russia/
- **Alaska Public / KUAC, 30 Jul 2007:**
  - Bushby and Kieffer had walked "about 400 miles into Siberia" in the spring.
  - The permit they believed was good for 6 months was good for only **2 months**, so he had to return to the US.
  - The two had split: Kieffer continued by bicycle.
  - Bushby planned to return to Siberia in early August, then renew his visa in Fairbanks in the autumn, then walk "Siberia's Kolyma River next winter".
  - https://alaskapublic.org/xx-statewide-news/2007-07-30/karl-bushby-heading-to-siberia-in-august-to-walk-home-to-the-uk
- **Gazeta.ru, 25 Feb 2011 (snippet; the page itself would not load):**
  - "В марте 2007 года путешественники вернулись на Чукотку … паводковые воды сильно размыли грунтовые дороги, превратив их в месиво. В мае путешественники приняли решение улететь домой."
  - The 2011 attempt is described as his "third attempt" on Chukotka, made alone.
  - https://www.gazeta.ru/social/2011/02/25/kz_3537185.shtml
- **Old Wikipedia text (snippet, via fandom/wikiwand mirrors):** "During 2007, he managed to walk more than 620 miles (1,000 km) from Uelen to Dvoynoye (not far from Bilibino), returning to Alaska when his visa ran out in November." This sentence carries [citation needed]. The current en.wikipedia has dropped it.
- **Route conflict:**
  - The 2007 plan (Egvekinot → Anadyr → Markovo → Magadan) differs from the route actually reported: Uelen → **Amguema** → Dvoynoye → Bilibino.
  - The Amguema–Dvoynoye–Bilibino route comes from the karlbushby.world snippet ("advancing only in winter, when frozen tundra and rivers could carry him").
  - **[INFERENCE]** He took the inland route via Amguema and the Iultin/Egvekinot area rather than the southern route via Anadyr and Markovo. Grokipedia (low reliability) says the route chosen to reach Yakutsk was about 800 km longer, to avoid summer swamps.
- **Abramovich's role (Times via Gazeta, snippet):** Governor Roman Abramovich personally helped Bushby get the papers to return. Grokipedia also mentions help with resupply (unverified).

### 2008: three weeks to Bilibino, then collapse
- 1sn.ru (28 Mar 2011): "В 2008 году Карл Бушби прервал своё путешествие по Чукотке в городе Билибино из-за распутицы."
- Old-Wikipedia snippet: "In 2008 he only walked for three weeks, reaching Bilibino."
- Russkiymir (25 Feb 2011): over the two years after the court case they "got only as far as Bilibino". https://russkiymir.ru/news/28038/
- In summer 2008 the financial crisis took away his sponsors (Vokrug Sveta; Holod).
- **Gap:** no exact 2008 start or end dates.

### Late 2008 to 2010: Mexico
- He lived cheaply in Mexico on friends' help and odd jobs (Vokrug Sveta; Wikipedia citing odysseyxxi.com). The Russian government issued a new visa in 2010 after new sponsors were found.

## 2. The 2011 season: Bilibino → Kolyma ice → Srednekolymsk

**Takeaway:** This was a solo winter traverse on winter roads (зимники) and Kolyma river ice, pulling a 70+ kg sled. It lasted about 46 days of walking, from 10 Mar to 25 Apr 2011, and covered about 1,100 km. On 26 March 2011 Chersky border guards wrote a protocol against him for being in the Nizhnekolymsky border zone without a permit. This is the most concrete documented "border-zone violation" and the most plausible basis for the later ban (see §4).

### Dates and places
- **25 Feb 2011:** flew by charter from Nome to Anadyr, then by air to Bilibino.
  - 1sn.ru (SakhaNews, author Aleksei Kurilo, 28 Mar 2011): https://1sn.ru/angliiskii-putesestvennik-zastryanet-na-kolyme
  - Russkiymir 25 Feb 2011 (citing Gazeta.ru, author Ekaterina Shorina): "Пересечь Чукотку можно только по зимним дорогам, пока реки не вскрылись ото льда." He had "about three months" to get from Bilibino to Zyryanka before the thaw.
- **10 Mar 2011:** left Bilibino for Anyuysk, about 300 km, which took 10 days.
- **22 Mar:** left Anyuysk for Chersky. Bad weather cost him two days wandering near the Lesakovka River.
- **26 Mar:** reached the Nizhnekolymsky district of Yakutia. «На Стадухинской протоке путешественника встретили военнослужащие пограничного поста "Черский". Проверив документы, они выяснили, что у него нет разрешения на пребывание в Нижнеколымском районе, что и было оформлено в виде протокола.» The article says this did not stop the trip.
- **Planned onward stops:** Kolymskoye → Andryushkino → Argakhtakh → Srednekolymsk → Zyryanka → Yakutsk. 1sn predicted he would not get out of Zyryanka before the thaw: "покинуть Зырянку до распутицы … вряд ли успеет".
- **25 Apr 2011:** reached Srednekolymsk after about 680 mi (1,100 km), ending the 2011 leg. Source: Wikipedia, citing odysseyxxi.com and an Ask Yakutia interview of 5 May 2011 (askyakutia.com is now a parked domain).

### Method, kit and conditions
- He pulled a sled of more than 70 kg at about 30 km/day and slept in a tent heated by a burner. He ate concentrated food, filmed and photographed, and carried a hot-water bottle.
  - 1sn.ru; KP-Irkutsk 28 Mar 2011: «За его плечами сани с грузом в 70 килограммов, а на улице мороз -25 градусов… спит в палатке, согревается грелкой». https://www.irk.kp.ru/online/news/858484/
- Locals: Galina and Anastasia Zimova (Chersky area) relayed contact with him for the journalist (1sn.ru).
- Wikipedia: his route "can only be travelled on foot via frozen rivers and ice roads, he could only walk during the late winter and early spring", on top of the 90-day visa limit.
- KP-Irkutsk also says he hoped to reach the London 2012 Olympics.
- **Conflict:** KP says he flew to Bilibino "в Магаданской области". Bilibino is in Chukotka; Gazeta-bam 2016 repeats the "Magadan" error.
- **Dating error to flag:** lenahotel.orgs.biz/news/148 reproduces the KP 2011 text (70 kg sled, −25 °C) under a date of **25 Mar 2015**. Treat it as a re-post, not 2015 evidence.

## 4. The border-zone violation and the ban

### What the sources say
- **Wikipedia:** "In March 2013, Russia banned Bushby … for five years, citing a border violation from the previous year". It cites Hull Daily Mail, 19 Mar 2013, by Kevin Shoesmith (not fetchable).
- **Holod (7 Aug 2025):**
  - In spring 2013 Russia banned him for 5 years for violating the border regime.
  - It links this to **a 2011 episode in Yakutia** where he "may have entered a closed zone without a required permit".
  - The ban was lifted in autumn 2014 with an official invitation.
  - "В марте 2015 года он вновь ступил на российскую землю."
  - https://holod.media/2025/08/07/britanecz-idet-vokrug-sveta-peshkom-uzhe-27-let/
- **Vokrug Sveta (2026):** the reason for the 2013 five-year ban was that "при пересечении Якутии он оказался в закрытой для иностранцев зоне без специального разрешения". https://www.vokrugsveta.ru/news/medvedi-beringov-proliv-i-abramovich-idushii-peshkom-vokrug-sveta-karl-bushbi-vernulsya-v-angliyu-spustya-28-let-id76688457/
- **Otello:** after Srednekolymsk he went to Mexico for a visa, and Russia again banned him for 5 years for entering a closed border zone without a special permit. https://otello.ru/nomer/kazhetsya-ty-zabryol-ne-tuda-kak-britanec-27-let-idyot-domoj
- **Jill Homer (Substack, 2025/26):** "In 2011, he inadvertently strayed into an off-limits zone and was again deported and banned." https://jilloutside.substack.com/p/the-longest-way-home
- **Men's Journal (May 2013, snippet):** Moscow banned him "in spring 2012" for five years. It cites "visa snafus and suspicion of espionage". Gazeta-bam 2016 adds that he told Russian journalists he had been treated as a foreign spy in Russia.
- **IOL (2026)** and **Lenta (2025)** attribute the ban to the 2006 Bering crossing. This conflates the 2013 ban with the 2006 case, in which the 5-year expulsion was annulled.

### Synthesis
- **[INFERENCE, well supported]** The "border violation" is almost certainly the **26 March 2011 protocol by the Chersky border post** for being in the Nizhnekolymsky district (a border zone on the Arctic coast) without a permit (1sn.ru).
- This matches Holod's "2011 in Yakutia" and Vokrug Sveta's "closed zone in Yakutia".
- Wikipedia's "previous year" wording does not fit 2011 if the ban came in 2013. It fits better if the ban decision actually dates to 2012, which is what Bushby and Men's Journal say.

### Dating conflict
| Event | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Visa refusal | 12 Apr 2012 | Wikipedia |
| Ban | "2012" | Bushby (Daily Beast 2015); Men's Journal "spring 2012" |
| Ban | March 2013 | Wikipedia, citing Hull Daily Mail 19 Mar 2013 |
| Ban | spring 2013 | Holod |
| Ban | 2013 | Explorersweb |

**Best reading:** visa refused in April 2012; the five-year ban became public in March 2013.

### Resolution
- He walked from LA to the Russian Embassy in Washington, about 2013–14. Distances quoted range from 3,000+ to 3,600 mi (4,800–5,800 km).
- Bushby says the campaign was partly initiated by producer **Jordan Tappis**, and National Geographic filmed it (Vokrug Sveta).
- The ban was lifted after a **Washington Post article (14 Aug 2014)** and a request from Tappis. Bushby felt guilty toward his helpers because he had half hoped to sit out the three remaining years (Vokrug Sveta).
- The new visa and letter of invitation came on **23 Oct 2014** (Wikipedia, Outside; Holod says "autumn 2014").
- The documentary *The Walk Around the World* aired on Nat Geo on **15 May 2015** (motionpictures.org).

## 7. 2017: to Mongolia
- He crossed the Russia–Mongolia border in 2017 and was in Ulaanbaatar on **8 Aug 2017**. Sources: Wikipedia, citing westboundhorizons (archived) and Hull Daily Mail; karlbushby.world (snippet).

## 8. Cross-cutting themes

### Method
- **Seasonality:** Chukotka and northern Yakutia could be walked only in late winter and early spring, on frozen tundra, winter roads (зимники) and river ice, before the spring thaw (распутица) and floods. Summer there is "impossible" (Vokrug Sveta; Wikipedia).
- **Gear:** in the north, a pulled sled of 70+ kg (2011). KP 2026 claims he moved "mostly on skis", which is unconfirmed by other sources. From 2015–16, on roads, a wheeled cart.
- **Partners:**
  - Kieffer only in spring 2007; solo afterwards.
  - No guides are documented.
  - **Snowmobiles:** documented only for the 2007 transfer from Anadyr to Uelen back to his start point. No evidence of snowmobile support while walking.
- **Transport between seasons:** he flew in and out (Nome↔Anadyr charters; Bilibino by air) and always resumed from the exact stopping point.

### Temperatures and dangers
- Documented figures:
  - −25 °C in late March 2011, Lower Kolyma (KP).
  - About −30 °C on the Bering Strait (Explorersweb).
  - Grokipedia claims about −62 °C in the taiga (unsourced; treat as unverified).
- **Bears:** the only documented encounter is a polar bear in the Bering/Alaska stage. No Siberian brown-bear incident was found.
- **Other dangers:** getting lost in weather (Lesakovka, 2011); spring floods (2007, 2008); the Aldan River swim (by 2016).

### Officials and locals
- **Officials:** border guards in 2006 (Capt. Kim) and 2011 (Chersky post protocol); the Lavrentiya and okrug courts; Governor Abramovich, whose intervention was reportedly prompted by John Prescott; the Russian Embassy in DC in 2014. He was suspected of espionage (Men's Journal; Gazeta-bam).
- **Locals:** Father Leonid (Lavrentiya, 2006); the Zimova family (Lower Kolyma, 2011); a school visit in Tompon district (2015); Neryungri rescuers and deputy Silin (2016).

### 90/180 rhythm
- A tourist visa allowed 90 days in any 180. He had to fly out (to Alaska before 2011; later to the US, Mexico or Canada) and back to his stopping point (Explorersweb; Holod; IOL).
- In 2007 the Chukotka permit lasted only 2 months.

### Mexico between seasons
- **Documented:** late 2008 to 2010 (Wikipedia; Vokrug Sveta, "about two years").
- **2013–14:** in the USA (protest walk).
- **2015–2017:** Explorersweb says that in general he "decamped to Mexico, Canada, or the U.S. every 90 days". No specific Mexico stay between 2015 and 2017 is documented.
- **[INFERENCE]** The off-season periods were most likely spent in Mexico or the US.
