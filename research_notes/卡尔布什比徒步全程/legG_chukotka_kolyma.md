## 3. Spring 2007 in detail: Uelen → Amguema with Kieffer [all NEW]

### Arrival, customs and officials (Kieffer, "Arrival in Anadyr", 16 Mar 2007)
Source: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2007/03/

**Arrival:**
- Flew Nome → Anadyr on Friday 16 Mar 2007 (Bering Air). Bering Air's local representative, **Olga Grokhotova**, met them with about five customs officers and border guards.
- They had **1,700 lb (~770 kg) of gear**, including **2.4 m sleds** and fuel tanks.
- Customs officer **"Igor"** recognised them from 2006 and asked "why are you back in Russia?".

**Customs duties and confiscations:**
- They were taxed **$880 each** for 160 kg of "consumer goods" (food) at €3/kg.
- Confiscated: **flare guns and flares**, which were their polar-bear deterrent, plus **GPS units, emergency beacons and satellite phones**. Only the GPS units were returned.
- The case went up to officials in Khabarovsk and the Ministry of Industry in Moscow.
- As backup bear deterrents they bought bangers and fireworks and kept bear mace.

**Registration fine:**
- They were fined **2,000 RUB each** for failing to register within 72 h in Ugolnye Kopi.
- The person who invited them was fined 14,000 RUB.
- This is a second 2,000-RUB fine, distinct from the 2006 court fine.

**People who helped:**
- Journalist **Svetlana Gobuleva**.
- Landlord and driver **Pavel Yashkin**. Lodging was a kommunalka in Ugolnye Kopi at 500 RUB per person per night.

### Preparations in Anadyr, 17 Mar – 5 Apr 2007 (Kieffer, 10 Apr 2007)
Source: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2007/04/

**Escort and route planning:**
- The official escort was Chukchi musher **Nikolai Ettyne** (from Neshkan). He did not have to walk with them, but had to be informed at every village. He also helped set the route.
- Search and rescue made them sign a letter guaranteeing payment for any evacuation flight by Chukotavia on the "Uelen–Omsukchan" trail.
- They met **Gema Gi-Ukai** of Chuvanskoye about a *summer* route Anadyr–Markovo–Omolon. He warned it would be swampy, full of bears and mosquitoes.

**Communications:**
- A **Globalstar** satellite phone was loaned by a government agency (Kieffer later calls it the "Chukotsnab" phone). It was for emergencies only, had one battery, and worked mostly in the mornings.

**Officials and other incidents:**
- Government official **John Mann** helped.
- Deputy governor **Andrei Gorodilov** sent a letter to all district administrations asking them to help.
- Kieffer was taken in for an hour's questioning by a border guard for photographing near the customs office.

**Temperature:** about **−30 °C** on an ice-fishing Sunday in late March near Anadyr.

### Planned scope
- Seattle Times (4 Apr 2007): a 1,630-mile trip from Uelen to Omsukchan, 135 days at ~15 mi/day.
- Kieffer (5 Apr): the target was "Uelen to Magadan".
- Restart was planned for 1 Apr; the actual restart was 12 Apr.

### Transfer back to the start line
- Kieffer, 5–10 Apr: a storm ("purga") halted the **вездеход** from Lavrentiya to Uelen. The town declared an "актированный день" (closed for weather), with winds of about 30 kn.
- Father Leonid explained that the drivers would not risk the mountains while carrying 5–6 female Uelen teachers.
- The regional administration provided the ride. The convoy was **3 vezdekhods**, with the third carrying **FSB** escorts. The sleds were strapped on top.
- **[CONFLICT]** tia-ostrova (20 Mar 2007) said they would go Anadyr → Uelen **by snowmobile**. What actually happened was a flight to Lavrentiya and then a tracked-vehicle (вездеход) convoy to Uelen. This is still a transfer to the 2006 stopping point.

### On the trail
- **Day 1 (12 Apr):** walked Uelen → Inchoun, ~30 km. The Inchoun mayor met them on a вездеход and hosted them, and they gave a school talk.
  - Kieffer's dispatch gives "about 6 degrees Celsius", which is almost certainly **−6 °C** **[INFERENCE]**.
  - **Sled weight: 240 lb (~109 kg) each.** This is much heavier than the 70 kg of 2011.
  - They spent the night of arrival in Uelen in the **police station**, the same place as in April 2006.
  - **[CONFLICT/typo]** The blog says they arrived "Wednesday, April 12th". 12 Apr 2007 was a Thursday, so the arrival was probably the evening of 11 Apr and walking began on the 12th.
- **Enurmino:** a storm kept them in their tents for about 36 h on the way. They repaired tents and gave a school presentation.
- **Neshkan, 24 Apr:** stayed an extra day on doctor's orders because Kieffer had open blisters. They took a phone call at the school principal's office.
  - They then abandoned the coastal winter road ("not saving them any real time") and **cut inland directly towards Amguema**, a section planned at about 3 weeks.
- **Separation, 7 May about 11:00:** a whiteout separated them before Amguema.
  - Kieffer found a winter road, reached a 50-man gold-mining camp the next day, and made 50 km/day down to Amguema, arriving on 11 May. The camp is spelled "Vastoshishno/Vastoshisno" and is not on his map. **[INFERENCE]** It may be "Восточное" or a similar name; it is unidentified.
  - Kieffer alerted the emergency department.
  - Bushby went straight across the tundra (estimated at ~20 km/day). A local on a snowmobile spotted him 9 km out, and he **reached Amguema on 14 May 2007**.
- **Permits:** at Amguema they learned their local **permits expired on 30 May 2007**. This matches Alaska Public's "only good for two months".

### The split at Amguema (Kieffer, 21 May 2007)
Source: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2007/05/
- On 14 May they met local policeman **"Sasha"** in Amguema and agreed to part. Kieffer's words: "Karl is now continuing on the Southern Chukotka route and I am planning to resume my expedition in January 2008 on the Northern Chukotka route."
- Kieffer left Amguema on 15 May. He walked the last ~90 km of road to **Egvekinot** in 24 h, arriving on 16 May. He handed his sled to firemen at km 66 because the road was snow-free.
- Kieffer left the Chukotsnab satellite phone with Karl.
- Editor's note: "Karl has chosen to continue on at this time through a separate route through the Spring."
- **Kieffer's tally:**
  - Uelen–Egvekinot: 684 km in 34 days.
  - His expedition-history page, in its Russian and French versions, says explicitly **"Уэлен – Ангуэм (с Карлом Бушби); Ангуэм – п. Эгвекинот (в одиночку)"**. The English page's "Anguema–Uelen (solo)" is a typo.
  - His partners page: "Karl Bushby February 2006 – May 2007 (1200 kms) From Nome, Alaska to Anguema, Chukotka".
  - Sources: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2008/01/expedition-history.html and https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2010/03/

**What this changes in seg3:**
- Bushby's spring-2007 endpoint was **Amguema, not Egvekinot**.
- The "about 400 miles" (Alaska Public) fits Uelen → Amguema, about 590 km by Kieffer's figures. Kieffer's own 684 km includes his ~90 km solo stretch Amguema → Egvekinot.
- Kieffer reached Egvekinot alone. Bushby never walked to Egvekinot, Anadyr or Markovo. Seg3's [INFERENCE] about an inland route via Amguema is now **confirmed for spring 2007**.

## 4. Autumn 2007 – 2008

### Autumn 2007: Amguema → Dvoynoye (weakly sourced)
- **Earliest appearance:** the sentence "during 2007 to walk more than 1000 km from Uelen to Dvoynoye (not so far from Bilibino) … permit ran out in November" first appears in en.wikipedia revision **260972220 (30 Dec 2008)**, with **no reference**. It was absent in revisions up to 21 May 2008.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=260972220
- **karlbushby.world (snippet only):** "Uelen into the Siberian interior — Amguyma, Dvoynoye, Bilibino — advancing only in winter".
- **No contemporary dispatch** for Aug–Nov 2007 was found. Kieffer's blog is silent on Karl after May 2007.
- **Distance check [INFERENCE]:** Uelen → Amguema is about 590 km. Amguema → the Dvoynoye deposit (upper Anadyr/Kupol area, Chukotka) is about 400 km straight-line. That makes "more than 1,000 km Uelen → Dvoynoye" plausible.
- **Bushby's own 2008 update** (below) says he returned in April 2008 and "made his way to the Dvoynoye mining camp and the start of the new walking phase". This confirms Dvoynoye as his 2007 stopping point.
- **[OPEN]** The exact autumn 2007 dates are unknown. It is also unclear whether "permit ran out in November" refers to a 2007 autumn season or garbles the spring permit.

### 2008: Dvoynoye → Bilibino [NEW dates]
Bushby's site update of **3 Jun 2008**, quoted in a forum post of 30 Jun 2008: https://forum.expeditionportal.com/threads/goliath-expedition.14276/
- "Karl returned to Russia on 16th April 2008 … made his way to the Dvoynoye mining camp and the start of the new walking phase."
- "Delays in obtaining a visa meant a late start this year and although the snow and conditions were good at first … the snow quickly began to melt and by the time he got to Bilibno on 18th May, Karl was dragging the sled over dirt."
- "Consequently, there was no chance of getting it to **Cherskiy**." This shows the 2008 target was Cherskiy via the Kolyma.
- "There was also the 90 day visa condition to consider and so he had to make the return journey to Anadyr and then the US, arriving in Nome on 26th May."
- The aim was to extend his allowed time in Russia before a "proposed return next winter". That return did not happen because the 2008 crisis cost him his sponsors.
- **[INFERENCE]** About 3 weeks of walking (late April → 18 May) matches Wikipedia's "only walked for three weeks". The Dvoynoye → Bilibino distance is not stated.
- **Escort context [NEW]:** Kieffer (Feb 2008) says that for 2008 Chukotka would no longer let him travel unescorted "as Karl Bushby and I were able to do last year". He had to hire a guide. Whether Bushby faced the same rule in 2008 is unknown. https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2008/02/

## 5. Late 2008 – 2010: Mexico gap [NEW nuances]
- **Alaska Public / KUAC, 19 Jan 2011:**
  - The expedition had been on hold "since the spring of 2008, when he left off in the remote mining town of Billibino". He spent **two years in Mexico** seeking sponsors.
  - He was in **Fairbanks** training, with a restart planned for **1 Feb 2011**.
  - A **$14,000 charter** from Nome would carry him and his gear.
  - Two TV producers were weighing a documentary or reality series. **[INFERENCE]** This was probably the seed of the later Nat Geo project.
  - Progress so far: 21,000 mi.
  - https://alaskapublic.org/2011/01/19/world-walker-renewing-trek-from-fairbanks
- **thelongestwayhome interview (24 May 2010):**
  - Expedition position "currently placed in Bilibino, Chukotka".
  - The road system was "900 miles of Arctic/Subarctic tundra" away.
  - He called the three-month visa limit "an impossible situation for an unfunded expedition on foot" and pinned hope on announced Russian visa liberalisation.
  - https://www.thelongestwayhome.com/blog/great-modern-travelers/interview-with-karl-bushby/
- **[CONFLICT]** On the distance remaining to roads, the 2010 interview gives **900 miles**. Wikipedia (2012), measuring from Srednekolymsk *after* the 2011 leg, gives **900 km / 560 mi**. These are consistent if 2011 covered about 1,100 km.

## 8. Cross-check summary: corrections to seg3

1. **The spring 2007 endpoint for Bushby was Amguema, reached 14 May 2007, not "west across Chukotka" generally.** Kieffer alone continued to Egvekinot.
2. **The 2007 transfer to Uelen was by вездеход from Lavrentiya**, with an FSB vehicle in the convoy, not by snowmobile from Anadyr. The walking start was 12 Apr 2007, not "~15–19 Mar". Mid-March was the arrival in Anadyr; then came ~3 weeks stuck over customs and storms.
3. **The 2007 sled weight was 240 lb (~109 kg) each**, against 70+ kg in 2011.
4. **2008 has exact dates:** in Russia 16 Apr; walking from late April from Dvoynoye; Bilibino 18 May; Nome 26 May. The target had been Cherskiy.
5. **Officials and locals added:**
   - Customs: Igor, Olga Grokhotova (Bering Air).
   - Escort: Nikolai Ettyne.
   - Deputy governor Andrei Gorodilov; John Mann.
   - Svetlana Gobuleva, Pavel Yashkin, Gema Gi-Ukai.
   - The mayor of Inchoun; policeman Sasha (Amguema).
   - Father Leonid again (Lavrentiya, 2007).
6. **Temperatures:** about −30 °C near Anadyr in late March 2007; about −6 °C (probably) at Uelen/Inchoun on 12 Apr 2007; −25 °C in the Lower Kolyma, late March 2011.
