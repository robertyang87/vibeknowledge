## 2. Canada (Alberta, BC, Yukon): new material

- **Graham Johnson (Edmonton) is a NEW person.**
  - RMOutlook (Banff), 5 Mar 2026, syndicated by Great West: "One of those strangers from the road was Edmontonian Graham Johnson. 'I met this crazy guy 20 years ago coming through Alberta'."
  - A friend who had met Bushby on a bike trip passed on Johnson's number. Bushby then called him.
  - Johnson: "I went and picked him up and we spent two weeks together and I helped him along the way and put some money in his pocket and some food in his little wagon that he had and sent him on his way."
  - Johnson adds that in the early days Bushby "would go days without eating because he didn't always have the money".
  - Source: https://hn.jakechampion.name/reader/https://www.rmoutlook.com/mountain-guide/he-refused-to-quit-fundraiser-for-man-on-27-year-walk-around-the-world-comes-to-banff-11944307 (reader copy; the original rmoutlook.com page returned 403).
  - **Gear note:** "little wagon" confirms he still pushed the wheeled cart (Beast 2) in Alberta.
  - **INFERENCE:** "Picked him up" for two weeks suggests a rest or resupply break near Edmonton. That would be on the Calgary–Edmonton–Grande Prairie–Dawson Creek line, but no source states where.
- **Correction to seg2 §5:** RMOutlook says the 2026 Alberta fundraiser series (Edmonton, Red Deer, Banff 7 Mar, Calgary) was organised by **Graham Johnson and "friends of Karl"**. Seg2 names Mark Scholz as host, from the Banff Centre listing. RMOutlook does not mention Scholz. Treat Johnson as organiser and Scholz as event host/MC (unverified).
  - Sam Roberts is described as having "been following Bushby's journey for the last couple decades".
  - The Red Deer talk was at the Margaret Parsons Theatre, $25. — https://rdnewsnow.com/2026/03/03/englands-karl-bushby-set-to-speak-to-red-deerians-about-27-year-expedition/
- **Year conflict persists:** RMOutlook says "By 2004, he crossed into Canada". Against this, the Salt Lake Tribune (18 Feb 2003), the Calgary Herald (11 Apr 2003) and Wikipedia's "a day away from crossing into Canada" (1 Jan 2003) all point to 2003. **Keep 2003.**
- **BC / Yukon / Whitehorse:** Still **no source** places him in Dawson Creek, Fort Nelson, Watson Lake, Whitehorse or Beaver Creek, and I found no Whitehorse Star or Yukon News text. The FDNM says only "after hiking up the Alaska Highway". The BC/Yukon transit remains INFERENCE (the road is the only route).
- **Calgary Herald, 11 Apr 2003:** A Wayback copy of the newspapers.com clipping exists (https://web.archive.org/web/20260208092648/https://www.newspapers.com/article/calgary-herald/190492561/), but it could not be fetched (connection reset). Text still a GAP.

## 3. Fairbanks arrival year: a direct conflict

- **FDNM (16 Dec 2005):** "Bushby showed up in Fairbanks in May **2003** after hiking up the Alaska Highway. He spent several months holed up in Fairbanks preparing for last winter's leg."
- **Bushby site (snippets):** Delta Junction diary 16 May 2004; "In May 2004, Karl walked into Fairbanks".
- **Assessment:** 2004 is preferred, and the FDNM year is probably a reporter's slip (INFERENCE).
  - He was still in Montana in mid-Feb 2003 and around Calgary in Apr 2003. Calgary to Fairbanks is about 3,000+ km, and he could not have covered it by May 2003 while pushing a cart.
  - The site says the Alaska Highway was walked "at forty below", which means a winter. That fits winter 2003–04.
  - "Several months … preparing for last winter's leg" (winter 2004–05) also fits a May 2004 arrival.

## 4. Alaska winter 1 (Dec 2004 to spring 2005): new details from the FDNM

Tim Mowry, "Ice breakers: Adventure duo plans Bering Strait crossing", Fairbanks Daily News-Miner, 16 Dec 2005. Full text as reposted by Kieffer: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2005/12/fairbanks-daily-news-miner.html

- **Original goal for 2004–05:** "last winter's leg of his journey from Fairbanks to Cape Prince of Wales on the Bering Sea coast."
- **Sled:** "Towing a sled laden with more than 200 pounds of food and gear."
- **Minto Flats:** "Bushby's decision to travel across the Minto Flats just outside of Fairbanks turned into a nightmare because of deep snow."
  - New place name. The Minto Flats lie west of Fairbanks toward Nenana and Minto, which fits the Dunbar diary entry of 23 Dec 2004 (INFERENCE).
- **Yukon River:** "The same was true for his trip down the Yukon River. Bushby ended up breaking trail through deep snow for days at a time."
- **Trooper resupply:** "At one point when he was camped on the 80-mile stretch between Tanana and Ruby, an Alaska State Trooper flew out and dropped off food and fuel to keep him going until he reached Ruby."
  - This puts **Tanana and Ruby** on his route for the first time from a source.
  - Galena and Nulato are still INFERENCE (they are the next river villages before Kaltag).
- **Bushby's verdict:** "I went out there pretty raw and ran into all sorts of problems … I learned an awful lot."
- **End of season:** "Bushby stopped in the Bering Sea coast village of Unalakleet before his visa ran out and he had to leave the country."
- **Meeting Kieffer, new detail:**
  - The Nome Nugget (Apr 2005, Janet Ahmasuk) lists among Kieffer's memorable moments "The practicing adventurers he met along the way, especially the one near Old Woman near Unalakleet." This is unnamed but clearly Bushby (INFERENCE, consistent with Nexus "old woman's cabin").
  - It also says: "Except for the adventurers in training, Kieffer never saw anyone walking."
  - Kieffer's dates: start Knik Lake 26 Feb 2005; Grayling to Kaltag took 5 days in a 75 mph headwind; finish under the Burled Arch in Nome on **Tue 5 Apr 2005** after 37 d 19 h and 1,049 mi.
  - INFERENCE: given those dates, the Old Woman meeting fell around mid-to-late March 2005.
  - Source: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2005/04/nome-nugget-alaska-april-2005.html
  - **Conflict on Kieffer's sled:** Nome Nugget "60 pounds of gear" vs Seattle Times 6 Apr 2006 "a 150-pound sled". The Seattle Times also gives 41 days, quoting his friend Don Wahl. — https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/20060406/dimitri06m/hes-an-amazing-guy
  - Nexus says the two "spent the rest of the year [2005]" preparing the 2006 crossing together. — https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2008/01/expedition-history.html

## 5. How he survived the winters: gear and method (new)

- **Winter 1 (2004–05):**
  - A 200+ lb pulled sled.
  - Deep-snow trail breaking (FDNM).
  - An air drop of food and fuel by an Alaska State Trooper between Tanana and Ruby (FDNM).
  - −51°C still air and losing 40 lb are already in seg2.
- **Winter 2 (2005–06) kit, as shown in Fairbanks on 15 Dec 2005:**
  - Black-and-yellow **Kokatat Gore-Tex kayaking dry suits**, **bunny boots**, floating plastic sleds towed while swimming, and ice axes clipped to the front of the suits.
  - "50 percent of our time on the ice and 50 percent in the water."
  - **MoroVision** helmet-mounted night-vision scopes (a sponsor) for travelling at night, so they would not lose ground to drift overnight.
  - A parafoil kite with a wireless camera feeding a hand-held screen.
  - GPS, satellite phone and emergency locator beacons.
  - A **shotgun and pistol** for polar bears, plus a motion detector on a ski pole around camp ("screams like a banshee").
  - Quotes:
    - "Our chances of making it are pretty slim … we'll be back next year and the year after that … This is my life."
    - "As far as we know nobody has ever made it from Alaska to Russia."
- **Kieffer's sponsor list (later Nexus page):** Morovision Night Vision, **Acapulka Sleds**, McMurdo emergency beacons, Beaver Sports (Fairbanks), Apocalypse Design (Fairbanks), Bering Air, Mountain House, Montrail, The North Face, Lynden International Logistics and others.
  - These are Kieffer's sponsors. Whether Bushby shared Acapulka sleds or the Fairbanks outfitters is UNVERIFIED (INFERENCE: plausible for the joint 2006 trip).
  - Source: https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2010/03/nexus-expedition-sponsors.html
- **Kieffer's practice (illustrative, not Bushby's):**
  - The tent pitched over the upside-down sled as its frame.
  - A thermos rather than a hydration pack, which froze.
  - Hydropel foot salve.
  - Mail-drop resupply at village post offices.

## 6. Norton Sound and Nome to Wales (Dec 2005 to Mar 2006): new details

- **Resumption point:** He flew back to **Unalakleet** (FDNM: "will pick up where he left off last year by flying to Unalakleet sometime in the next week"). Then Iditarod Trail coast route, about 220 mi to Nome.
  - INFERENCE: Shaktoolik, Koyuk, Elim, Golovin, White Mountain, Solomon. Only Shaktoolik is dated (30 Dec 2005).
- **Nome to Wales villages:**
  - Kieffer: "Having spent time in the Alaskan villages of Teller and Brevig Mission on my way from Nome to Wales last year." — https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2007/03/from-anchorage-to-nome.html
  - So the route was **Nome, Cape Woolley, Teller, Brevig Mission, Wales**. Brevig Mission is new.
- **Villagers:**
  - **Joe Garnie**, "a well-respected Inupiyak musher in Teller", hosted Kieffer in his snow-buried sweat lodge, Feb 2006. — https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2013/05/yakutsk-russian-federation-innermost.html
  - Kieffer later gave away "my traditional Alaskan Inupiyak hat from Brevig Mission, made out of spotted seal" in Chukotka (2010). — https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2010/03/in-middle-of-2500-reindeers.html
- **School talks:** "Goliath expedition presentation", Jan–Feb 2006, at schools in Teller, Brevig Mission and Wales. — https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/2008/10/expedition-presentations.html (also the French list at /2011/07/les-presentations-de-lexpedition-nexus.html)
- **Drift and helicopter:** No new primary text found. Still open, as before:
  - Exact date (diary: 2–5 Feb).
  - Where they were flown. Seattle Times 3 Jun: Kieffer was treated for frostbite in Nome.
  - Where they resumed. INFERENCE: back at the Cape Woolley shoreline, per the expedition's "unbroken line" rule. Not sourced.
  - The Evergreen name still rests only on the site snippet.
  - No independent ADN, Nome Nugget or KNOM report of the rescue was found. A Nome Nugget story on a March 2006 drifting-ice rescue of gold miners is a different event; do not conflate.
- **Kieffer's Flickr album "Bering Strait Crossing 2006":** 60 photos, including "BBC Pictures taken at start in Wales" and "Complete Nome - Wales - Uelen route". No visible captions for Teller, Woolley or the helicopter. — https://www.flickr.com/photos/nexusexpeditions/albums/72157634762948675/
