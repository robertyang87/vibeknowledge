# Verify: Bering 2006 / Russia fine / ban / DC walk / start funds
Checked 2026-11-05. Blocked: karlbushby.world (Vercel checkpoint/429), archive.org (no access), WaPo/Outside/Backpacker (paywall/403).

## 1. Helicopter, 2006 Bering Strait: CONFIRMED (helicopter used); no evidence it carried them forward
- Seattle Times, 6 Apr 2006 ("He's an amazing guy", Kieffer story): ice "had broken loose and they had drifted 28 miles out to sea"; "They had to be rescued by helicopter". https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/20060406/dimitri06m/hes-an-amazing-guy-
- Seattle Times, 3 Jun 2006 (Kieffer interview): tent on ice block "broken free in a storm"; "They drifted for 36 hours until rescued by a helicopter they called in with their satellite phone"; "Kieffer went to Nome to be treated for frostbite and later reconnected with Bushby to continue their walk to Russia"; flew Russia to Nome on 24 May. https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/20060603/dimitri03m/much-suspense-near-tip-of-the-world
  - So the rescue took them BACK to Alaska/Nome, and they restarted from there. The drift figures (28 mi vs 36 h; site says ~30 km) differ.
- Explorersweb 2026: "rescued by helicopter after becoming stranded on an ice floe that broke away from the coast". This puts it in the Alaskan stage. https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-is-home-after-28-years-on-the-road/
- Search snippet of Bushby's site (/story, could not fetch): "driven thirty kilometres out to sea on floating ice and had to be lifted off by helicopter".
- Filson (2025, narrative): during the crossing "they were resupplied once by plane and again by helicopter". This was a supply drop: the pilot landed briefly and then left, because he could not follow them into Russian airspace. https://www.filson.com/blogs/journal/profiles-the-bering-strait-crossing
- The 2026 AP copy DOES mention the helicopter (Melley/Super; PBS, NBC, La Nacion):
  - "he was rescued by helicopter from an ice floe drifting out to sea"
  - "After being airlifted to safety during the 56-mile (90-kilometer) Bering Strait crossing in 2006, Bushby and American adventurer Dimitry Kieffer started again ..."
  - https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/karl-bushby-embraces-his-mother-as-he-completes-his-28-year-walk-around-world ; https://www.nbcnews.com/world/united-kingdom/karl-bushby-completes-28-year-31000-mile-walk-world-returns-mothers-ho-rcna606998 ; https://www.lanacion.com.ar/usa/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home-nid31102026/
- The successful crossing ran 17-31 Mar 2006 from Wales (AK) to Uelen (Kieffer's Nexus profile: "Nome to Wales (US) to Uelen (Russia)"). Detained around 1 Apr. No source found of a helicopter flying them from Uelen to Lavrentiya. How they got from Uelen to Lavrentiya is UNRESOLVED.
- Verdict: a helicopter rescue is CONFIRMED. It was a rescue followed by a restart, which AP also says ("started again"), not a forward lift. A supply helicopter is also reported mid-crossing. Kieffer's own blog (nexusexpedition.com) is now 404, so no first-hand blog text was found. Wikipedia omits the helicopter.

## 2. Fine, April 2006: both numbers are in print; the underlying amount is 2,000 RUB each
- Rossiyskaya Gazeta, 6 May 2006: first-instance court imposed "штрафу в 2 тысячи рублей и административному выдворению". The Chukotka okrug (district) court removed the deportation and kept the fine. https://rg.ru/2006/05/06/puteshestvenniki.html
- AP/NBC, 14 Apr 2006 and Seattle Times, 15 Apr 2006: "$72 each". https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna12315893 ; https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/20060415/deport15m/world-trek-threatened-by-russian-court-order
- Reuters/Interfax via Cape Argus, 5 May 2006: "$75 (about R495) for each foreigner". https://capeargus.co.za/news/world/2006-05-05-russia-ends-threat-to-deport-adventurer/
- Verdict: 2,000 rubles each is CONFIRMED. "$72" (AP) fits the April 2006 rate of about 27.7 RUB/USD; "$75" (Reuters) is a looser conversion. Prefer "2,000 rubles (about $72)".
- The appeal ruling came on Friday 5 May 2006 (RG says "вчера" in its 6 May issue).

## 3. 2012 refusal and five-year ban: date partly resolved, reason UNRESOLVED
- 12 Apr 2012: Bushby reported a visa refusal on his site (Wikipedia, citing the homepage).
- Wikipedia: "In March 2013, Russia banned Bushby ... for five years, citing a border violation from the previous year". Explorersweb, Holod ("весной 2013"), Vokrug Sveta and Unilad all say 2013.
- Bushby himself (Daily Beast first-person, 15 May 2015): "It was 2012 ... a five-year visa ban that had just been slapped on me". https://www.thedailybeast.com/my-epic-walk-around-the-world
- Reasons given:
  - Russian media (Vokrug Sveta, Holod, Otello): a border-regime violation, i.e. entering a closed border zone without a special pass ("в закрытой для иностранцев зоне без специального ... разрешения"). Vokrug Sveta places this in Yakutia. https://www.vokrugsveta.ru/news/medvedi-beringov-proliv-i-abramovich-idushii-peshkom-vokrug-sveta-karl-bushbi-vernulsya-v-angliyu-spustya-28-let-id76688457/ ; https://holod.media/2025/08/07/britanecz-idet-vokrug-sveta-peshkom-uzhe-27-let/
  - Lenta.ru ties the 5-year ban to the 2006 crossing, which conflates it with the 2006 case. Note that the 2006 deportation was overturned.
  - No source found for a "2011 visa-regime violation". No primary FSB or court document found.
- Verdict:
  - Refusal in April 2012: CONFIRMED.
  - Formal five-year ban: most secondary sources say March/spring 2013, but Bushby writes "2012". Best phrasing: "visa refused April 2012; five-year ban imposed (reported March 2013)".
  - Reason: "border(-regime)/border-zone violation" is the consensus wording. Linking it to 2006 is unsupported. The Yakutia closed-zone detail comes from Russian popular media only.
  - Ban lifted: 23 Oct 2014 (Wikipedia, Outside); Holod says "осенью 2014".

## 4. Washington DC protest walk: route CONFIRMED; dates and distance UNRESOLVED
- Route: Los Angeles to the Russian Embassy in Washington DC (AP 2026, Wikipedia, Explorersweb).
- Distance varies by source:
  - AP "by the numbers": 3,500 mi
  - Wikipedia and Unilad: "over 3,000 miles (4,800 km)"
  - Explorersweb: about 5,000 km
  - Site snippet: 5,600 km
  - Longestwayhome (2014): "3000 miles"
- Timing:
  - Men's Journal (May 2013) reportedly gave a planned September 2013 start (article 403, seen only via search snippet).
  - Outside: "spring of 2014, as Bushby neared the final leg of his U.S. trek"; a WaPo piece (14 Aug 2014, "Will Russia thwart Karl Bushby's epic hike around the globe?") prompted the embassy to respond; new visa on 23 Oct 2014.
  - Otello/Holod: started "весной 2014-го". This conflicts with the above.
- No source found giving the exact start day or embassy-arrival date. AP's "spent a year walking" implies roughly 2013-2014.
- Verdict: route CONFIRMED. Start date, arrival date and exact distance are UNRESOLVED. Do not state a specific date. Safe wording: "2013-14, about 3,000+ miles (4,800-5,600 km)".

## 5. Starting funds: $500 CONFIRMED as the mainstream figure
- $500 sources:
  - AP 2026: "He set out with $500" (La Nacion)
  - Hull Story: "$500 US dollars"
  - Explorersweb: "around $500"
  - Stars and Stripes, Box Life, Briefly
- $800 sources: Holod ("На старте у него было 800 долларов") and thelongestwayhome ("little more than $800", which also notes it could have been $500).
- Other figures: Otello says $300; Lenta says "несколько сотен долларов".
- No pound-sterling figure found.
- Verdict: use "$500 (US dollars)". Treat $800 as an outlier.
