## 2. China / Xinjiang 2018: new details

- Through search snippets, the official story page names **Urumqi**. It describes Xinjiang as the most heavily monitored part of the whole route, with "**cameras, biometric checkpoints, and tailing vehicles around Urumqi**".
  - This is the first source to place him at Urumqi, i.e. on the main G30/G312 corridor west to Khorgos. [INF on the road]
  - Source: https://karlbushby.world/story (snippet)
- Two versions now exist **[CONFLICT, minor]**:
  - Wikipedia mirrors and older revisions say he was "**detained** by Chinese authorities… released after negotiations involving the British Embassy".
  - The current Wikipedia text says "progress temporarily restricted".
  - The defunct WestBound Horizons page (snippet) says police tried to block him, "forcing him to leave China", until "with a lot of luck the Chinese police and Karl could come to an agreement". It does not mention the embassy.
  - The official site gives the fullest account: the tyre-shack stop, three plainclothes groups, a hotel under armed guard, then the embassy deal (see seg4 §3).
- **Exit crossing: still unsourced for Bushby.** The parallel evidence below points to **Khorgos** [INF, medium]:
  - Baikal (the same STTW project, following him later) waited **five months at the Chinese border** to get in. By Nov 2018–Jan 2019 she was at "**the Khorgos border crossing between China and Kazakhstan**, waiting for permits… since November". — https://explorersweb.com/interview-with-baigalmaa-norjmaa-the-mongolian-camel-lady/
  - Urumqi plus a walking route to Almaty points to Khorgos (Nur Zholy) rather than Bakhty/Tacheng or Alashankou/Dostyk. Bakhty would bring him out near Ayagoz, far north of Almaty.
- **Dates:** still no entry or exit date. The window is between Jan 2018 (when STTW broke up) and roughly Nov 2018 (the OnTheGoSolo red line). [INF]
- No named Chinese officials, and no named British embassy officer. [NOT FOUND]

## 3. Kazakhstan, late 2018 to May 2019

- The Russian-language feature by Otello (2025) summarises the stage only as "Бушби вернулся в Россию, добрался до Монголии, **прошёл Казахстан и Туркменистан**" ("Bushby returned to Russia, reached Mongolia, crossed Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan"). It gives no towns. — https://otello.ru/nomer/kazhetsya-ty-zabryol-ne-tuda-kak-britanec-27-let-idyot-domoj
- OnTheGoSolo: a Facebook message of **5 Jul 2019** gave his plan from Almaty "south through Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan, then walk across the northern edge of Iran toward Türkiye". OnTheGoSolo dated the route to after leaving Almaty. — https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/
- **Rika:**
  - YouTube "**Central Asia with Rika**" (8 min 1 s) was published **8 Apr 2026**. Its whole description is "**2018-2019**", which dates the time they spent together in Central Asia. — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfVTnkR61Ms
  - Combined with the official site ("met up with Rika in Almaty"), this means Rika joined him in Kazakhstan in 2018 or 2019 and stayed through Uzbekistan.
  - Her surname and nationality are not given. "Japanese" rests only on AP's "a woman from Japan". [INF]
- **Winter:**
  - Baikal says she spent "almost four months at each border". Bushby probably wintered in or near Almaty in 2018–19, though no source says so. [INF]
  - It is not known whether he walked through the winter.
- Towns (Taraz, Shymkent, the border post): **[NOT FOUND]**.
  - The road south-west from Almaty to Uzbekistan via Shymkent ends at **Zhibek Zholy–Gisht Kuprik** north of Tashkent.
  - If he entered at Gisht Kuprik, Tashkent would be the first Uzbek city. [INF, unverified]

## 4. Uzbekistan, June 2019

- Provenance of the "early June 2019" date: it was added to Wikipedia on **11 Jul 2019** from an IP address in **Tbilisi**. It is therefore not necessarily Bushby's own statement, though it is uncontradicted. — https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/
- On 12 and 17 Jul 2019 OnTheGoSolo still could not place him inside Uzbekistan.
- Samarkand and Bukhara with Rika (official site) is already in seg4. Nothing new on Tashkent or Navoi. [NOT FOUND]
- Uzbek outlets have no 2019 coverage. Daryo.uz (2025) covers only the finish, and its page returned 403.
- Distance check:
  - Bukhara to the Turkmen border at Alat/Farap is about 100 km.
  - From Farap the road runs via **Turkmenabat → Mary → Tejen → Ashgabat**.
  - He told Dave's Travel Corner that re-entering at Uzbekistan meant "losing almost 700 miles". That roughly equals Bukhara→Ashgabat→border, about 1,000–1,100 km. [INF, consistent]

## 5. Turkmenistan, July–August/September 2019

New from the official site (snippets):
- "Turkmenistan's visa rules forced Bushby to **cross alone**." Rika did not enter Turkmenistan; the break-up with her came around this point. [INF]
- He had a strict **30-day visa** with a **mandatory 24-hour government escort**.
- "His escorts carried most of his gear while he walked the **Karakum Desert** toward the Iranian border."
- "Turkmenistan never reopened to him, Iran stayed out of reach, and the planned northern reroute through Russia ended with the 2022 invasion of Ukraine."
- Source: https://karlbushby.world/story

Other details:
- Otello (RU) says Iran "flatly refused" him in 2019. He then tried for a Russian document to walk via **Astrakhan Oblast** (around the north Caspian) and was refused "once again". — otello.ru
- His father, Keith, wrote on 27 Aug 2019: "Everything hinges on getting around the Caspian Sea… Russian authorities were just about tolerating him." — OnTheGoSolo
- Iran-border post: still unsourced. The Karakum framing plus "hurried toward Ashgabat" points to **Gaudan/Bajgiran** (Ashgabat–Quchan road) [INF]. Turkmen exile media (Turkmen.news, Azatlyk, Chronicles of Turkmenistan) have no coverage. [NOT FOUND]
- Named Turkmen officials or escorts: **[NOT FOUND]**.
- Exit: by **12 Oct 2019** he was in a bar in Mexico (Facebook). How he left Turkmenistan is not described, nor whether by air from Ashgabat. "Forced to leave" (ExplorersWeb 2021) implies he flew out. [INF]

**Arrival date at the Iran border [CONFLICT, unchanged]:** 27 Aug 2019 (Keith Bushby) versus 15 Sep 2019 (Karl, El Informador). A possible way to reconcile them [INF]:
- Suppose entry from Uzbekistan in late July and a 30-day visa.
- 27 Aug would then be arrival at the border zone.
- 15 Sep may be his remembered date for the final stop or departure, though it would exceed 30 days unless the visa was extended.
- This is unresolved.

## 6. People index (Leg I)

- **Baigalmaa "Baikal" Norjmaa**:
  - From Ulaangom; founder and leader of Steppes to the West (STTW).
  - Continued alone through Xinjiang (five-month wait at the Chinese border) and Khorgos (Nov 2018 onward).
  - By about 2019–20 she was in Turkmenistan and Iran, with 5,400 km covered (mongolia-trips.com).
  - **Unverified:** a Wikitia page with a notability flag claims she died in May 2021 in a ger fire. No corroboration was found; **do not use without confirmation**. — https://wikitia.com/wiki/Baigalmaa_Norjma
- **Angela Maxwell**: on STTW from Aug to mid-Dec 2017. No new detail.
- **Russell and Tara** (Australian Camels): camel trainers. Already in seg4.
- **Rika**: with him in Central Asia, 2018–2019 (YouTube description). Joined in Almaty. Did not enter Turkmenistan (official site: "forced to cross alone").
- **Keith Bushby** (father): source of the 27 Aug 2019 status report.
- Unnamed: Baikal's younger brother (a policeman); Chinese plainclothes police; the British Embassy in Beijing (no officer named); the Turkmen government escorts.
