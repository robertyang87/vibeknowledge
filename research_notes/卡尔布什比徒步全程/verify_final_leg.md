# Verification: final leg of Karl Bushby's Goliath Expedition (checked 2026-11-05)

## 1. PM message read by Hull East MP: CONFIRMED
- UK PM in Nov 2026 is **Andy Burnham**, in office since 20 Jul 2026 after Keir Starmer resigned. Sources: NBC https://www.nbcnews.com/world/united-kingdom/andy-burnham-british-prime-minister-keir-starmer-labour-king-charles-rcna588324 ; NPR https://www.npr.org/2026/07/19/g-s1-134001/andy-burnham-new-british-prime-minister ; CNBC https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/uk-prime-minister-burnham-trump-poverty-stricken-disaster.html
- PA (Dave Higgens) via Yahoo UK: Hull East MP Karl Turner "read out a message of congratulations" from "Prime Minister Andy Burnham": "To walk around the world from Chile and back again is an achievement of truly remarkable proportions." https://uk.news.yahoo.com/adventurer-karl-bushby-mum-asks-174146760.html
- Irish Examiner (PA) adds: "It speaks of courage, resilience, perseverance and unbreakable spirit." It also names the Lord Mayor as Maria Coward. https://www.irishexaminer.com/world/arid-41932203.html
- Speakers: Lord Mayor Cllr Maria Coward gave the civic welcome: "For 28 years – 10,229 days – Hull has been waiting for your return." Karl Turner MP read the PM's message. Bushby spoke. No other MPs are named.
- Al Jazeera, NBC, CBS and The Hull Story do not mention the PM message. They don't contradict it either.

## 2. Guinness World Records: UNRESOLVED (no evidence of certification)
- No GWR page, certification or application was found. A search of guinnessworldrecords.com returned nothing for "Bushby".
- CBS quotes Bushby: "We've had to create two world records just to get back here." The records and the certifying body are not named. https://www.cbsnews.com/news/englishman-karl-bushby-returns-home-walking-goliath-expedition/
- Media use the phrases "longest continuous walk in human history" (The Hull Story) and "longest unbroken foot trek". These are not GWR titles.
- Wikipedia does not mention Guinness.

## 3. Number of countries: UNRESOLVED (reported totals 29 to 30; "30" is not settled)
- **29**: BBC (Kevin Shoesmith, 5–6 Oct) "29 countries" https://sg.news.yahoo.com/karl-bushby-completes-english-channel-183428972.html ; Fox News https://www.foxnews.com/travel/veteran-walked-across-four-continents-swam-english-channel-return-home ; The Hull Story "four continents and 29 countries" https://www.thehullstory.com/allarticles/karl-bushby-homecoming
- **30**: NBC "four continents and 30 countries" https://www.nbcnews.com/world/united-kingdom/karl-bushby-completes-28-year-31000-mile-walk-world-returns-mothers-ho-rcna606998
- One search summary said the official site lists 25. I could not verify this because karlbushby.world returned HTTP 429.
- Route as compiled from the sources:
  - Americas: Chile, Argentina (Wikipedia), Peru, Ecuador, Colombia, Panama, then Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Honduras, El Salvador, Guatemala (named only as the planned route in later retrospectives, not individually confirmed), Mexico, USA, Canada.
  - Asia: Russia, Mongolia, China, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan, Georgia (Daily Sabah: "entered Türkiye through Georgia in February" 2025).
  - Europe: Turkey, Bulgaria, Romania, Hungary, Slovakia (Wikipedia, Dec 2025), Austria, Germany, Belgium, France, UK.
  - Counting all of these gives 32. The reported 29/30 totals therefore probably leave out some of them (for example Argentina, El Salvador or Slovakia, or count transit only). This was not reconciled.
- **Not on the route:** Netherlands and Luxembourg (no source). Iran (visa refused; he swam the Caspian instead). Armenia appears in one secondary source only and is probably an error.

## 4. Channel swim: "3 days" CONFIRMED; Wikipedia's "approximately four days" REFUTED
- BBC (via Yahoo): "swam for 20 hours over three days". He made landfall at Shakespeare Beach, just south of Dover, at 15:30 BST on Monday 5 Oct 2026. He was greeted by his father Keith Bushby and Werner Herzog. Explorersweb, Fox and Archyde agree on 3 days and about 20 h. Explorersweb adds that he spent the nights on a safety vessel and restarted each morning from his last position. https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-is-home-after-28-years-on-the-road/
- Start date: not published. The team kept it private for safety. Working back from the 3 days gives about 3 Oct. Wikipedia mentions a planned date of 1 Oct. No source names the start point (for example Cap Gris-Nez).
- Eurotunnel refusal (mid-June 2026): "We have carefully considered his request and explored a range of possible options... Closing the Service Tunnel for a period of 15 hours, or over two days, would pose a safety risk." It also said "regretfully we are unable to accommodate Mr Bushby's wishes." https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-refused-entry-into-channel-tunnel/ (dated 19 Jun 2026). Permission to swim came from the French authorities.

## 5. Belgium arrival: UNRESOLVED, best estimate late May to early June 2026; distance after Dover: CONFIRMED about 250 mi
- Explorersweb (19 Jun 2026): "In May, Bushby finished walking through Germany and entered Belgium."
- Wikipedia and other secondary sources say "early June". One says he "entered Belgium in the past week".
- He then returned to Mexico (visa/Schengen reset). He resumed in Belgium in September, reached Tournai on 18 Sep and entered France on 20 Sep (Wikipedia; Euro Weekly News 7 Aug said he planned to return in early September).
- Distance remaining at Dover: Fox (citing the BBC) says "roughly 250 miles" (about 400 km) to Hull.

## 6. Hull arrival, crowd, route: PARTLY CONFIRMED
- Date: Monday 2 Nov 2026.
- Archyde, citing the BBC: "By Sunday [1 Nov], he had crossed the Humber Bridge into East Yorkshire." https://archyde.com/karl-bushby-completes-31000-mile-28-year-journey-to-hull
- The Hull Story: he walked in from Hessle along Hessle Road and Anlaby Road. He reached the bottom of Carr Lane "just before 11am", then went into Queen Victoria Square for the civic welcome outside City Hall.
- PA: a piper led him to City Hall. He then walked about 3 miles via North Bridge to his mother's house in the Sutton Park area, under a "Welcome Home Son" banner.
- Crowd: "hundreds" (PA, Al Jazeera, The Hull Story, Explorersweb). No source gives an exact figure.
