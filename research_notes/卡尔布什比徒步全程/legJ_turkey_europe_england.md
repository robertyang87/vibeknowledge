## 3. Turkey: Black Sea towns (NEW, previously unattested)

**East Black Sea leg (IHA via Yeni Akit, 12 Feb 2025)**
- Town-by-town: Sarp Border Gate → Kemalpaşa → Hopa → Arhavi (Artvin) → enters Rize at **Fındıklı** → **Ardeşen → Pazar → Çayeli → Rize city centre → Derepazarı → İyidere** → crosses into Trabzon province → **Of** district (by 12 Feb 2025).
- Quotes: "Burada iyi zaman geçiriyorum" ("I'm having a good time here"). On continental crossings: "you must either walk or swim".
- He says he met many people in every district, drank tea with them, and stayed overnight in a small district. That hospitality slowed him down.
- He still hoped to use the **Channel Tunnel** to reach England.
- Source: [Yeni Akit/IHA, 12 Feb 2025](https://www.yeniakit.com.tr/haber/26-yildir-yuruyerek-dunyayi-gezdi-rizede-iyi-zaman-geciriyorum-1915346.html)
- **Pace [INF]:** Sarp (5 Feb) to Of (~12 Feb) is about 130 km in about 7 days.

**Planned route [CONFLICT with actual]**
- In Feb–Mar 2025 he said he would go via **Greece**, then Bulgaria, Hungary, Austria and Germany (Yeni Akit, 12 Feb). NTV (13 Mar) also says "walk to Greece, then England".
- In the event he went from Edirne straight into Bulgaria via Kapıkule, so Greece was dropped. **[INF]** Greece was probably dropped for routing or Schengen-timing reasons; no source explains why.

**Gaps and pace**
- **Trabzon city, Giresun, Ordu, Ünye and Fatsa** are not named in any source found. **[INF]** They lie on the D010 coast road between Of and Samsun, so he almost certainly passed through them, but this is unattested.
- **[INF]** Of (~12 Feb) to Samsun (≤13 Mar) is about 380 km in about 29 days, which is slow. That fits his comments about heavy rain, snow and hospitality.

**Samsun (≤13–14 Mar 2025)**
- **Samsun Metropolitan Municipality hosted him in its boutique hotel.** He said: "Seyahat ettiğim 26 yıl boyunca böyle bir şey hiç olmadı" ("In the 26 years I've been travelling, nothing like this has ever happened").
- Also: "Samsun oldukça güzel bir şehir" ("Samsun is quite a beautiful city"). Fellow travellers had warned him: "Türkiye'de misafirperverlikle boğulacaksın" ("You'll drown in hospitality in Turkey").
- He said the first weeks in Turkey were hard: heavy rain, cold, snow.
- He claimed **39,000 km** walked. **[CONFLICT]** Milliyet (22 Apr) gives 47,000 km for the same leg; these are presumably different counting bases.
- Sources: İşçi Haber; NTV/DHA; [Haber Gazetesi](https://www.habergazetesi.com.tr/dunya-turu-yapan-gezgin-samsunda-sok-oldu-boyle-bir-durumla-hic-karsilasmadim)

**Samsun → Bolu → Istanbul**
- No towns were found for Samsun → Bolu (Amasya, Çorum, Havza, Merzifon, Osmancık, Tosya and Kastamonu were all searched). **[INF]** The D100/D795 corridor via Havza, Merzifon, Osmancık, Tosya and Gerede to Bolu remains the likeliest. That is about 400 km, which fits 13 Mar → 16 Apr.
- **NEW: Kocaeli, 22 Apr 2025** (Milliyet). "Şu anda İstanbul'un hemen yakınlarına kadar geldim" ("I've now come right up close to Istanbul"). He was waiting for permission for the bridge or a swim.
  - "Boğazı geçtikten sonra evime sadece 1 yıl kalmış olacak" ("Once I cross the Bosphorus, I'll be only a year from home"). This was accurate within about 6 months.
  - "Türkiye'de çok güzel karşılandık" ("We were welcomed very warmly in Turkey").
  - About 47,000 km done; about 13 of the 26 years actually walking.
  - Source: [Milliyet, 22 Apr 2025](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/26-yildir-yuruyen-adam-istanbula-geliyor-bogazi-gectikten-sonra-evime-1-yil-kalmis-olacak-7355968)
- **[INF]** The route was therefore Bolu → Düzce → Sakarya → İzmit (Kocaeli) → Gebze → Üsküdar. This is the standard D100 line; only Kocaeli is attested.
- Bridge crossing on 2 May: also covered by [Türkiye Gazetesi](https://www.turkiyegazetesi.com.tr/yasam/kitalararasi-yuruyuste-tarihi-an-15-temmuz-sehitler-koprusunu-de-gecti-1112743) and [NTV gallery](https://www.ntv.com.tr/dunya/galeri-tarihi-an-ingiliz-gezgin-15-temmuz-sehitler-koprusunden-yuruyerek-avrupaya-gecti,JbzIMA06tEGGMNcuJYhZoQ).

## 9. Schengen 90/180: worked explanation (updated)

**The rule.** UK nationals may spend up to 90 days in any rolling 180-day window across the Schengen area. Bulgaria and Romania have been full Schengen members since 1 Jan 2025, so his Schengen clock started at Kapıkule.

**Stint A:** 1 Sep 2025 (enters Bulgaria) → day 90 = **29 Nov 2025**.
- Reuters: Komárom on 25 Nov. Győr (~40 km) is about 2 days on.
- He exits at about day 89–90. **Resolved.** Türkiye (non-Schengen, its own 90/180 rule) explains Mexico break #1 separately.

**Break #2:** about 30 Nov 2025 → mid-Mar 2026 (more than 90 days outside, so the clock fully resets).

**Stint B:** about mid-Mar 2026 (Tulln on 24 Mar) → 90 days ≈ mid-Jun 2026.
- Belgium on 31 May; the TikTok sign-off about "7 days in Belgium" ≈ 7 Jun. **[INF]** He exited around 7–14 Jun.

**Stint C [INF]:** for an exit about 3–5 Oct, the 180-day look-back starts about 7–9 Apr. Days used from ~8 Apr to ~10 Jun ≈ 63–67, which leaves about 23–27 days. So re-entry could be **no earlier than about 8–12 Sep 2026**.
- This fits: the BBC Havré→Mons walk (~15–18 Sep), Tournai on 18 Sep, France on 20 Sep, Blanc-Nez around 25 Sep, and the swim 3–5 Oct.
- It explains why he could not simply "return in early September" as Euro Weekly News predicted, and why the late-September permit scare ("semi-panicked") was critical: he had very few Schengen days in hand.
