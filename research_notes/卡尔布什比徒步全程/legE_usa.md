## 1. NEW findings (not in seg2)

### 1.1 Utah is confirmed: Salt Lake City (new)
- **Citation:** Wikipedia ref. [19] gives the full citation: Janet Rae Brooks, **"Man Who Visited SLC on His Walk Around the Globe Has His Trailer Stolen"**, *Salt Lake Tribune*, **18 Feb 2003, p. D3**. Clipping: https://www.newspapers.com/article/the-salt-lake-tribune/189953723/ (403). Citation from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby
  - The headline says he **visited Salt Lake City** on the walk. That puts Utah on the route, and almost certainly the I-15 corridor (INFERENCE).
  - The date, 18 Feb 2003, suggests the theft story was still running in mid-February. That fits the "about one month" in Helena and the recovery of the cart from the quarry just before he left the US (INFERENCE).
- **Earlier visit coverage:** A Salt Lake Tribune or Deseret News article from when he actually passed through SLC (probably around Oct 2002, INFERENCE) was not found.

### 1.2 Tucson and Superfeet: Karl's own account (new detail)
Source: interview with Dave's Travel Corner (Teh Chin Liang), 9 Nov 2022 — https://www.davestravelcorner.com/interviews/karl-bushby-adventurer-and-author/

- **How Superfeet came in:** "In Tucson, Arizona, I found a shoe store with a Superfeet rep."
  - So the sponsorship began through a chance meeting with a sales rep at a Tucson shoe store, not through a formal approach.
- **The hosting company:** "At the US border we were contacted by a Canadian website hosting company."
  - So the hosting sponsor made contact at the US border (around late Apr 2002), not later in the year.
  - The company is still unnamed (GAP).
- **What the money meant:** "These were small amounts of money but a huge deal for someone who had no idea how I could survive in North America."
- **Before the US:** "Before the US border I never had any financial support other than some handouts."
- **Latin America versus North America:** "You can survive in Latin America with a pocket full of rice, that's not so easy in North America."
- **Why sponsors appeared now:** "the US had a huge outdoor market unlike Latin America at that time."
- **Superfeet detail [unverified snippet]:** A search-engine summary says "a separate blog by Karl" states that Superfeet "supplied him with two sets of insoles". I could not locate that blog. Treat it as unverified.

### 1.3 Desert heat and route wording from the site [site-snippet]
- **Heat and route:** He crossed "the American southwest in temperatures above fifty degrees Celsius, [then] north into Nevada and up through Montana".
  - A second page on the site says "fifty-degree heat" with no unit.
  - **Flag:** 50 °C is near the record for the region. 50 °C ≈ 122 °F is possible around Parker or Lake Havasu in June–July, but extreme. Treat it as approximate.
- **Parker diary, 13 Jun 2002:** The entry opens with "**High forties again, not a tree in sight.**" This is new wording: high 40s °C, which fits Parker in June.
- **Flag on the same snippet:** It also contains "a thermometer by the cabin door reads -45°C" and snow-buried timber. That is clearly spliced from a different (Arctic) entry by the snippet engine. Do not attribute it to Parker.
- **Theft location:** The site again says "The Beast 2 was stolen from outside a café near the Canadian border" in Montana.
- **Sources:** https://karlbushby.world/story ; https://karlbushby.world/

### 1.4 Men's Journal 2013 timeline [snippet only; the page is 403]
- **Montana:** He "is about to cross from Montana into Canada when he decides to step into a bar to ring in the New Year … He leaves the Beast outside, where it is promptly stolen."
  - The section header reads **"New Year's Eve 2003"**.
  - **Conflict:** The header year probably means the night of 31 Dec 2002 going into 2003. UPI (3 Jan 2003) fixes the night as Tuesday 31 Dec 2002.
- **Bar or café:** Men's Journal says bar, agreeing with UPI. The site says café. That is now 2 sources to 1 for a **bar** (the Four Corners Bar, per UPI).
- URL: https://www.mensjournal.com/travel/karl-bushby-is-walking-around-the-world-no-really-20130514

### 1.6 Visa arithmetic (INFERENCE)
- Entry on 29 Apr 2002 plus 90 days (a Visa Waiver–type stay) gives an expiry of about **28 Jul 2002**.
- The Arizona Daily Star story "Take a hike…" ran on **26 Jul**, so he learned of the problem at or just before expiry while in Las Vegas.
- The visa could not be extended from inside the US, hence the flight to Bogotá. The resumption story ran on 20 Aug, so the restart at Las Vegas was around mid-August.
- The new **six-month** visa, issued around mid-Aug 2002, would run to about mid-Feb 2003.
  - This explains the pressure to leave the US soon after the January rebuild in Helena.
  - The quarry recovery came "just before he was ready to depart the United States".
  - It also fits the SLT story dated 18 Feb 2003.
  - INFERENCE: he crossed into Alberta around **mid-to-late Feb 2003**. That is consistent with the Calgary Herald story placing him near Calgary by 11 Apr 2003.

## 4. Conflicts flagged
1. **Bar or café (Shelby):** UPI and Men's Journal say bar; karlbushby.world says café. Prefer **bar** (Four Corners Bar).
2. **Night of the theft:** Men's Journal says "New Year's Eve 2003", UPI says Tuesday 31 Dec 2002, and Wikipedia says New Year's Day 2003. These are reconcilable: the night of 31 Dec 2002 to 1 Jan 2003.
3. **Heat figure:** "above fifty degrees Celsius" versus "fifty-degree heat" (no unit), both [site].
4. **Parker diary snippet:** contaminated with an Arctic passage (−45 °C). Use only the "High forties" line.
5. **Who recovered the cart:** police (derivative site only) or unspecified (Wikipedia).
6. **When the hosting sponsor appeared:** "at the US border" (Karl, 2022) versus "in 2002" (site). These are compatible; the border date is more precise.
