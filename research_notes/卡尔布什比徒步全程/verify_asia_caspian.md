# Fact-check: Central Asia, Caspian, Caucasus legs (checked 2026-11-05)

## 1. Caspian swim, 2024
| Sub-claim | Verdict | Best value |
|---|---|---|
| Start place: coast near Aktau, Kazakhstan | CONFIRMED | near Aktau |
| Start date | CONFIRMED | **16 Aug 2024** (it was planned for that day and the start was reported that day) |
| Finish in Baku | CONFIRMED | **17 Sep 2024, 18:30** (Bushby's own stats post) |
| Days | MIXED: 31 is the expedition's official figure | **31** (Bushby, AP, BBC, official site). ExplorersWeb gives 32 days at sea and 27 of them swimming. **41 is wrong**: AzerTAc's figure, which Kazakh outlets copied |
| Distance | 288 km is the standard figure; Bushby's own stats post says 275.5 km | 288 km (179 mi) |
| Return to the exact point after sleeping on the support boat | CONFIRMED as the method, with no GPS log made public | — |

Evidence:
- Tengrinews, 15 Aug 2024 (https://en.tengrinews.kz/people/an-american-a-briton-and-azerbaijanis-decided-to-swim-across-263827): the swim was set to start Aug 16 from Aktau, cover about 300 km in about 30 days at about 10 km a day, with an Azerbaijani warship and two vessels in support.
- OnTheGoSolo tracker, which quotes Bushby's Facebook posts (https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/):
  - 16 Aug 2024: "The swim leg is officially underway."
  - Bushby's stats: "Task Complete: 18:30 – 17/9/2024 … Distance: 275.5km … Hours swam: ~132hrs 21min … Days: 31".
  - 14 Feb 2023 plan: swim about 10 km a day, "sleep on a support boat at night. Each morning, they will begin again at the same place they got out of the water."
- ExplorersWeb, 19 Oct 2024 (https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/): "a 288km swim across the Caspian Sea"; "Over 32 days at sea, they spent 27 days swimming"; "They swam for three hours in the morning and three hours in the afternoon and slept aboard the boats."
- Backpacker (2026), as quoted in search results: he rested "on a support boat at night and return[ed] to the coordinates where he had gotten out of the water in the morning."
- Ynet (2026), on the Channel: he resumed "from the exact point where he had stopped, in accordance with the rules he had established for his wider expedition."
- AzerTAc, 17 Sep 2024 (https://azertag.az/ru/xeber/vsemirno_izvestnyi_puteshestvennik_nam_povezlo_s_pogodoi-3184406): "288 km … 41 days." The same figure appears in Idman.biz (17 Sep), Lada.kz and Azh.kz (18 Sep), and Newtimes.kz (19 Sep). Counted from 16 Aug to 17 Sep, the swim lasted 32 to 33 calendar days, so 41 cannot be right.
- AP, 31 Oct 2026 (Korea Times reprint, https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home): "forced to swim 31 days across the Caspian Sea", wearing a flotation vest and wetsuit.
- Error to note: ExplorersWeb, 28 Apr 2025, says the swim began "in October 2024". That is wrong.

## 2. Iran visa refusal and the Turkmenistan crossing
- **Refused in 2019: CONFIRMED. Refused in 2018: REFUTED.** He stopped at the Turkmenistan–Iran border.
- Dates: he crossed into Uzbekistan in early June 2019, and his father Keith posted on 27 Aug 2019: "As of today, Karl Bushby is on the border of Turkmenistan and Iran." That puts the Turkmenistan crossing in roughly July to August 2019. I found no exact date for entering Turkmenistan. He then had to leave the country when his Turkmen permit expired; ExplorersWeb says escorts took his equipment.
- ExplorersWeb, 5 May 2021 (https://explorersweb.com/2021/05/05/where-in-the-world-is-karl-bushby/): "By the end of 2019, he had trekked through Turkmenistan to its border with Iran … But he couldn't get a visa into Iran." Also: "stuck in Turkmenistan, on the Iranian border"; "Turkmen government escorts confiscated his equipment and forced him to leave the country."
- The "2018" version comes from ExplorersWeb, 28 Apr 2025: "In 2018, he crossed Kazakhstan and then made his way through Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan." That sentence runs the years together. The primary posts date the Turkmenistan part to 2019.

## 3. Russia–Ukraine war as a reason Russia was not an option
**CONFIRMED.** Bushby says so himself, and the media repeat it. Note that Russia was already effectively closed to him: he had a 2013 five-year entry ban, and Russian visas had always been hard for him to get.
- Big Issue, 14 Aug 2026 (https://www.bigissue.com/news/karl-bushby-walk-around-the-world/), Bushby: "Then Russia moving into Ukraine was a major obstacle and I ended up having to swim the Caspian".
- OnTheGoSolo, 6 May 2022 update: little had changed, apart from "Russia invading Ukraine and making that area of the world even more difficult to travel near."
- Times of Central Asia, 8 Aug 2024 (https://timesca.com/caspian-sea-swim-by-travelers-carl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell/): the swim was a response to "the current climate which prohibits crossing Russia and Iran by foot."
- VisasNews (2026): Russia was no longer viable, "especially after the invasion". CBS, via Yahoo, also says the swim avoided the war in Ukraine (seen in search results only).
- Other accounts don't mention the war: AP calls it "geopolitical problems", and The Hull Story says "chances of getting a Russian visa were negligible."

## 4. Xinjiang/China, 2018
- **He entered China in 2018: CONFIRMED (low precision).** I found no exact entry or exit dates.
- **Police escort: CONFIRMED in substance.**
  - AP, 31 Oct 2026: "He was trailed by police through China."
  - Wikipedia, citing the official site: he entered Xinjiang in 2018, "progress was temporarily restricted by local authorities … following intervention by the British Embassy he was allowed to continue under restrictions and police escort." Some mirrors say "detained".
  - ExplorersWeb, 28 Apr 2025: he left the Mongolian camel caravan and "continued into China by himself", then in 2018 "crossed Kazakhstan".
  - The official story page (karlbushby.world/story) was behind a bot checkpoint and I could not read it. Search snippets describe detention and his phone being taken there.
- **Dates: UNRESOLVED.** The 2018 crossing out of China into Kazakhstan is supported only at the level of the year.

## 5. Azerbaijan to Georgia
- **Exact border-crossing date: UNRESOLVED.** I found no source giving the day he entered Georgia.
- Brackets:
  - He left Baku on about 9–10 Nov 2024. He posted "Leaving Baku and headed west" on 9 Nov 2024, and the official site gives 10 Nov 2024.
  - On 30 Jan 2025 he posted a video from Batumi after "a long month walking along busy highways", as he was about to enter Türkiye.
  - He entered Türkiye at Sarp on about 6 Feb 2025 (Türkiye Today).
- Walking Baku to the Georgian border is about 450 km, so he most likely crossed into Georgia in **late Nov or Dec 2024**. That is an inference, not a sourced date. "Early 2025" is wrong for entering Georgia; it is the date he left Georgia.
