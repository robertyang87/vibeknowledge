# Karl Bushby's Goliath Expedition: Russia, Asia and the Caspian (2006 to 2025)

Scope note: These notes cover the period from the Bering Strait crossing (March 2006) to Bushby's entry into Europe at Istanbul (May 2025). They also include a few later facts where they help with context. Several sources found in search were not fetched or could not be fetched; these are marked "(search snippet only)". BBC pages could not be fetched (tool blocked). The Washington Post 2014 profile returned a 403 error. The westboundhorizons.com page (his former official site) now redirects to a gambling domain, so it was not used.

## 1. Exact dates and places for each stage, 2006 to Turkey/Europe

### Takeaway
After the March 2006 Bering crossing, Bushby made very slow progress in Chukotka and Siberia, limited by visas and weather. Bureaucracy stalled him from 2008 to 2010, and again from 2012 to 2014 through a visa denial and ban. He resumed in Siberia in 2015 (Yakutsk), reached Mongolia in 2017, then crossed China in 2018 and Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan in 2019. He stopped at the Iranian border in about September 2019. COVID and the Iran refusal kept him based in Mexico until 2024. He then backtracked to Uzbekistan, walked the Kyzylkum, swam the Caspian from the Aktau area to Baku (August to September 2024), walked Azerbaijan and Georgia, entered Turkey on 5 February 2025 and crossed the Bosphorus into Europe on 2 May 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Bering Strait and Chukotka (2006)**
- Bushby first planned to cross the frozen Bering Strait in winter 2005. He only got as far as Unalakleet, Alaska, before spring came and his US visa ran out. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- From 17 to 31 March 2006, Bushby and French adventurer Dimitri Kieffer crossed from Alaska to Siberia on foot. The strait is 58 miles (93 km) wide, but their 14-day route covered about 150 miles (240 km) of frozen sea. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
  - Conflicting details: AP (via Korea Times, 2026) calls Kieffer "American" and gives the strait as 56 miles. AP also says they were "airlifted to safety" and nearly lost their supplies after a plunge into freezing water. — [Korea Times/AP](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home)
- In early April 2006, Russian border troops detained both men near Uelen, Chukotka, for not entering at a proper port of entry. They faced a ban that would have ended the expedition. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby), citing [Spokesman-Review, 5 Apr 2006](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2006/apr/05/adventurers-held-by-russians-after-crossing/); see also [Seattle Times, 15 Apr 2006](https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/20060415/deport15m/world-trek-threatened-by-russian-court-order) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna12315893) (search snippets only)
- On 5 May 2006, a Russian appeal court cancelled the expulsion order and the walk continued. Reports credited talks between UK Deputy PM John Prescott and Roman Abramovich, then governor of Chukotka. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby), citing [Guardian, 5 May 2006 (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20230416030808/https://www.theguardian.com/uk/2006/may/05/russia.world1); see also [Cape Argus, 5 May 2006](https://capeargus.co.za/news/world/2006-05-05-russia-ends-threat-to-deport-adventurer/) (search snippet only)

**Stall in Mexico (late 2008 to 2010)**
- From late 2008 to 2010, Bushby lived in Mexico to save money. He could not go to Russia because of visa and permit problems, and he lost his sponsors in the 2008 financial crisis. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)

**Siberia, 2011 leg**
- He resumed walking in Russia in spring 2011, after finding new sponsors in 2010 and getting a new visa. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- On 25 April 2011 he reached Srednekolymsk (Yakutia), about 680 miles (1,100 km) into that year's leg, and ended the 2011 leg there. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- Weather only allowed travel for a few months each year, and AP says getting through Russia took "almost 10 years". — [Korea Times/AP](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home)
- His tourist visa allowed only 90 days in every 180 in Russia, so he had to leave the country regularly. — [ExplorersWeb, 28 Apr 2025](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-begins-home-stretch-of-27-year-round-the-world-walk/)

**Visa denial and ban (2012 to 2014): see Section 2**
- On 12 April 2012 he said Russia had refused him a 2012 visa. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- In 2013 he walked more than 3,000 miles (4,800 km) across the US, from Los Angeles to the Russian Embassy in Washington, DC. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby), citing [Victor Valley Daily Press, 14 Sep 2013](https://www.vvdailypress.com/story/news/2013/09/14/walk-around-world-stops-by/37155085007/)
  - Conflicting figures: AP gives 3,500 miles (5,600 km) over about a year (search snippet only, [NBC Washington/AP](https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/national-international/epic-28-year-walk-karl-bushby-by-the-numbers/4175433/)). Wikipedia also says his son Adam joined part of "the 2014 US protest walk", which suggests the walk ran from 2013 into 2014. No exact date of arrival in DC was found.

**Return to Siberia (2015 to 2017)**
- On 15 May 2015 Bushby wrote in the Daily Beast that he was in Yakutsk, Russia, "back on track, headed southwest", and that the visa had come through days after the paperwork was filed. — [Daily Beast, 15 May 2015](https://www.thedailybeast.com/my-epic-walk-around-the-world) (search snippet; my full-page fetch returned only part of the text)
- In 2017 he crossed the Russia–Mongolia border and was in Ulaanbaatar on 8 August 2017. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- On 16 October 2017 an Instagram post showed him with the "Steppes To The West" team, which planned to trek from Ulaanbaatar to London with 6 people and 10 camels. By January 2018 most of the team, Bushby included, had "gone their separate ways". — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/)
- He met American walker Angela Maxwell while crossing the Gobi Desert. — [ExplorersWeb, 19 Oct 2024](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/)

**China and Central Asia (2018 to 2019)**
- In 2018 he entered China and went through Xinjiang, where local authorities held him up for a time. After the British Embassy stepped in, he continued under restrictions and a police escort, then entered Kazakhstan. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby); AP says police trailed him in China — [Korea Times/AP](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home)
- He went from Almaty, Kazakhstan, towards Uzbekistan and crossed into Uzbekistan in early June 2019. — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- His father, Keith Bushby, said that by 27 August 2019 Karl had walked through Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan and was at the Turkmenistan–Iran border. In a February 2020 interview with El Informador, Bushby said he reached the Iranian border on 15 September (2019). — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/)
  - Conflicting dates: ExplorersWeb (Apr 2025) puts the Turkmenistan crossing and the Iran visa failure in 2018, and says the pandemic "set in around this time". The 2019 dates from OnTheGoSolo and Wikipedia are better supported. — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-begins-home-stretch-of-27-year-round-the-world-walk/)

**Stall, 2019 to 2024: see Section 3**

**Backtrack and Kyzylkum (2024)**
- To restart, Bushby and Maxwell flew to Bukhara, Uzbekistan, and walked across the Kyzylkum Desert to the Caspian. — [ExplorersWeb, 28 Apr 2025](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-begins-home-stretch-of-27-year-round-the-world-walk/); [ExplorersWeb, 19 Oct 2024](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/)

**Caspian swim (August to September 2024): see Section 4**

**Azerbaijan and Georgia (September 2024 to February 2025)**
- After landing in Baku in September 2024, Bushby walked west through Azerbaijan towards Turkey for about six months. — [ExplorersWeb, 28 Apr 2025](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-begins-home-stretch-of-27-year-round-the-world-walk/)
- A travel blog says he left Baku for Turkey on 10 November 2024 (search snippet only, source not identified, so treat as unverified).
- A Facebook post put him in Batumi, Georgia, just before he crossed into Turkey. He described "a long month walking along busy highways" in rain and icy conditions (search snippet only).
- No source found gives his Azerbaijan–Georgia border date or his route through Georgia.

**Turkey and entry into Europe (February to September 2025)**
- On 5 February 2025 he entered Turkey through the Sarp Border Gate on the Georgian border (Kemalpaşa, Artvin) and walked about 20 km to Hopa on the first day. He said it was day 9,584 of the walk. He was 55, with about 6,200 km left. He planned to follow the Black Sea coast to Samsun and then turn inland to Istanbul. — [Daily Sabah/Anadolu, 7 Feb 2025](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/british-adventurer-enters-turkiye-on-foot-during-world-journey/news) (a photo caption on the same page dates the Artvin photo to 6 February)
- He passed through Samsun on the way. — [NTV (Turkish)](https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/26-yildir-hicbir-ulasim-araci-kullanmadi-guney-amerikadan-yola-cikip-yuruyerek-samsuna-geldi,SoG-AkXFIUuNtEWMTUHPIw) (search snippet only)
- On 2 May 2025 he walked across Istanbul's Bosphorus Bridge into Europe, his first time in Europe since 1998. His 90-day Turkish visa was almost used up, so he went to the airport the same day. He waited about three months in Mexico before he could re-enter Turkey, then landed back in Istanbul around 19 August 2025. — [ExplorersWeb, "Back in Europe"](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-back-in-europe-begins-last-leg-of-journey/); see also [Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/british-adventurer-arrives-in-istanbul-after-26-year-walk-208670) (search snippet only)
- He crossed from Turkey into Bulgaria at the Kapıkule gate (Edirne). Sources give 1 September 2025; Wikipedia says September 2025. — [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/british-traveler-praises-turkiyes-hospitality-after-completing-walk/news) (search snippet); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- Context after this period: Romania (October 2025), Budapest (15 November 2025), the Channel swim (5 October 2026), and arrival in Hull (2 November 2026). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)

### Inferences
- The Russian part of the walk (2006 to 2017) went in short seasonal pieces: Chukotka in 2006 and later years, the Kolyma/Srednekolymsk area in 2011, Yakutsk in 2015, and the Mongolian border in 2017. That fits AP's "almost 10 years" through Russia.
- The 90-day Schengen and Turkish visa rules forced him to fly out to Mexico several times in 2025 and 2026. Mexico was his usual waiting base in 2008–10, 2019–24 and 2025–26.

### Gaps
- I found no exact locations or dates for the Chukotka winter legs in 2006–2008 (e.g. Anadyr, Bilibino). The Wikipedia text I fetched skipped from May 2006 to late 2008.
- I found no exact dates or towns for the 2015–2017 legs from Yakutsk to the Mongolian border (whether via Irkutsk or Buryatia is unverified).
- I found no exact date of arrival in Washington, DC.
- I found no date for the Azerbaijan–Georgia crossing or for the route through Georgia.

## 2. Russian visa problems: why banned and how resolved

### Takeaway
Russia had been hostile since his unannounced Bering crossing into Chukotka in 2006. It refused him a visa in 2012 and gave him a five-year entry ban in 2013, citing an earlier border violation. He protested by walking from Los Angeles to the Russian Embassy in Washington, and the ban was lifted in about 2014. By May 2015 he was back walking from Yakutsk.

### Cited Findings
- In 2006 he was detained for entering Russia away from an official port of entry. An expulsion order was overturned on appeal on 5 May 2006. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- On 12 April 2012 Russian authorities refused him a visa for 2012. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- In March 2013 Russia banned him from re-entering for five years, "citing a border violation from the previous year". — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby), citing [Hull Daily Mail (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140203182708/http://www.hulldailymail.co.uk/Russia-ban-Hull-s-round-world-walker-Karl-Bushby/story-18460279-detail/story.html)
  - Conflicting dates: the Daily Beast (2015) dates the five-year ban to 2012. AP (2026) only says Russia "denied Bushby a visa" in 2012, and that the protest walk "eventually led to the proper paperwork". The most likely reading is a visa refusal in 2012 followed by the formal ban in 2013. — [Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/my-epic-walk-around-the-world); [Korea Times/AP](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home)
- Bushby's father said in 2019 that Russian authorities and Karl "have never been the best of friends" since he arrived unannounced, and that they were "just about tolerating him". — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/)
- Bushby's producers joked that he could "walk to the Russian embassy in DC from LA", and he did. — [Daily Beast, 15 May 2015](https://www.thedailybeast.com/my-epic-walk-around-the-world)
- How it was resolved: ExplorersWeb says the ban was lifted in 2014, after "years of diplomatic efforts", and that he got permission to walk across Russia in 2014. — [ExplorersWeb, Apr 2025](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-begins-home-stretch-of-27-year-round-the-world-walk/); [ExplorersWeb, "Back in Europe"](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-back-in-europe-begins-last-leg-of-journey/)
  - A search snippet (source not clearly identified) says that at the end of the US walk the ban was overturned and he was given a letter of invitation from the Russian government and a visa at the embassy in 2014. Wikipedia only says the ban was "later overturned" and gives no date.
- A Washington Post Magazine profile (14 Aug 2014), titled "Will Russia thwart Karl Bushby's epic hike around the globe?", covers this period. It could not be fetched (HTTP 403). — [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/lifestyle/magazine/will-russia-thwart-karl-bushbys-epic-hike-around-the-globe/2014/08/14/6c6d120a-0c59-11e4-8c9a-923ecc0c7d23_story.html)

### Inferences
- The "border violation" behind the 2013 ban may be the 2006 unauthorised entry, or a later problem with his visa or route. Wikipedia's phrase "from the previous year" points to something in 2012, but the AP wording ("initial border violation") points to 2006. This is unresolved.

### Gaps
- I found no official Russian statement of the reason (e.g. from the FSB or Border Service).
- It is unclear whether British diplomatic help or US media pressure was decisive in lifting the ban.

## 3. The pandemic and the war in Ukraine: route changes and why Russia was blocked

### Takeaway
Iran refused Bushby a visa in 2019, and his British passport made one "practically impossible". He went back to Mexico in autumn 2019 and stayed there through the COVID-19 pandemic, about five years in all. Going north around the Caspian through Russia was already unlikely in 2019 because of his history there. Later sources say geopolitics (Russia and Iran) had closed both land routes, and Wikipedia says both countries were "too dangerous for a British passport holder". In 2022 he and Maxwell began planning a Caspian swim as a workaround.

### Cited Findings
- His first request for an Iranian visa (summer 2019) was refused. A tourist visa would have needed "protracted negotiations", and his British passport made one "practically impossible". In February 2020 he said: "Iran is the last country: if I pass it I will get home." On 10 March 2020 he posted about his months-long struggle for permission to enter Iran. — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/)
- He was already in Mexico by 12 October 2019 (a Facebook post), had a birthday in Cihuatlán, Mexico, on 1 December 2019, and was still in Mexico on 11 January 2020. — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/)
- ExplorersWeb says he flew to Los Angeles to work out a new plan, and then the pandemic grounded him. — [ExplorersWeb, 19 Oct 2024](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/)
- The journey stopped when Iran refused the visa and COVID-19 hit, and he "returned to Mexico for several years". — [ExplorersWeb, "Back in Europe"](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-back-in-europe-begins-last-leg-of-journey/); Wikipedia says the pause at the Turkmenistan–Iran border was due to "visa issues and the COVID-19 pandemic". — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- In July 2019, walking north around the Caspian would have meant going back into Russia. OnTheGoSolo judged that unlikely given his past trouble getting permission to walk there. — [OnTheGoSolo](https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/)
- The Caspian swim was planned "to avoid Iran and Russia", which Wikipedia calls "too dangerous for a British passport holder" at the time. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- AP describes the swim as made necessary because "geopolitical problems" had closed off Russia and Iran. — [Korea Times/AP](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home)
- The swim was conceived in 2022 as a backup after Iran and Russia blocked his route, and planning took about two years. — [ExplorersWeb, Apr 2025](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-begins-home-stretch-of-27-year-round-the-world-walk/)

### Inferences
- The 2022 start of planning and the "too dangerous for a British passport holder" wording match the period after Russia's full invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. However, no source I fetched names the Ukraine war explicitly as the reason the Russian route was blocked. Treat that link as inference.
- Afghanistan does not appear in any source as a route he considered.

### Gaps
- No fetched source discusses the Ukraine war explicitly. The FCDO "advise against all travel" to Russia and Iran would be the likely context, but I did not verify it.
- I found no source on what Bushby did in Mexico during 2020–2023 (work, residence, documentary filming).

## 4. Caspian Sea swim: dates, distance, support, method and partners

### Takeaway
In 2024 Bushby, American walker Angela Maxwell, and two teenage Azerbaijani swimming champions swam about 288 km from near Aktau, Kazakhstan, to Baku, Azerbaijan. They swam two 3-hour sessions a day from support boats and slept on board, and arrived in Baku around 17 September 2024. Sources disagree on the duration: 31, 32 or 41 days.

### Cited Findings
- **Distance:** 288 km (179 miles). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby); [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/); [AzerTAc, 17 Sep 2024](https://azertag.az/ru/xeber/vsemirno_izvestnyi_puteshestvennik_nam_povezlo_s_pogodoi-3184406). Before the swim, the plan was "approximately 300 km" from Aktau to Baku, starting mid-August and taking about a month. — [Tengrinews](https://en.tengrinews.kz/people/an-american-a-briton-and-azerbaijanis-decided-to-swim-across-263827); [Kazinform](https://www.inform.kz/ru/iz-kazahstana-v-azerbaydzhan-britanskiy-puteshestvennik-planiruet-pereplit-kaspiyskoe-more-62d8d4) (search snippets)
- **Start and finish:** It started from the Kazakh coast near Aktau and finished in Baku, where Deputy Minister of Youth and Sports Mariana Vasilyeva and the Azerbaijan Swimming Federation met them. — [NewTimes.kz, 19 Sep 2024](https://newtimes.kz/mir/194645-izvestnyi-puteshestvennik-pereplyl-kaspiiskoe-more-iz-aktau-v-baku); [AzerTAc](https://azertag.az/ru/xeber/vsemirno_izvestnyi_puteshestvennik_nam_povezlo_s_pogodoi-3184406)
- **Dates (conflicting):**
  - Start: August 2024 according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby). A search snippet says it was "officially underway on August 16, 2024", but the source was not identified.
  - Finish: the AzerTAc report from Baku is dated 17 September 2024, and NewTimes.kz republished it on 19 September 2024. A Fox News caption says September 2024 (search snippet).
  - ExplorersWeb (Apr 2025) says the swim was in "October 2024". This is wrong, since its own swim article was published on 19 October 2024 and describes the swim as finished.
- **Duration (conflicting):** Wikipedia gives 31 days and 132 swimming hours. ExplorersWeb gives 32 days at sea with 27 swimming days. AzerTAc and NewTimes.kz give 41 days. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby); [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/); [AzerTAc](https://azertag.az/ru/xeber/vsemirno_izvestnyi_puteshestvennik_nam_povezlo_s_pogodoi-3184406). (A start in early August and a 17 September finish fits 41 days. A 16 August start fits 31–32 days.)
- **Method:** They swam two 3-hour sessions a day (morning and afternoon) and rested and slept on support boats each night. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby); [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/). Bushby wore a flotation vest and wetsuit and called his technique "floatimming". — [Korea Times/AP](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261031/in-28-years-of-walking-karl-bushby-has-overcome-jungle-desert-and-red-tape-now-hes-nearly-home). Wikipedia and ExplorersWeb ("Back in Europe") describe the swim as done "in stages".
- **The "resume at the same GPS point" rule:** No fetched source states it explicitly. Bushby's general rule is no transport, and no going home until he finishes. — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/)
- **Partners:** Angela Maxwell, an American who spent 7 years walking about 32,186 km through 14 countries on 4 continents and who admits she is "not a natural swimmer". Also Anastasia Boborykina (16 years old; spellings vary: Boborkina, Boborikina) and Abdurrahman Rustamov, both 15-time Azerbaijani champions. — [AzerTAc](https://azertag.az/ru/xeber/vsemirno_izvestnyi_puteshestvennik_nam_povezlo_s_pogodoi-3184406); [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby)
- **Support and organisers:** Azerbaijan's Ministry of Youth and Sports, the operating company "Baku City Ring", and the Azerbaijan Swimming Federation. Safety boats escorted them throughout. Pre-swim coverage mentioned Azerbaijani naval support (search snippet, not confirmed in the fetched AzerTAc text). — [NewTimes.kz](https://newtimes.kz/mir/194645-izvestnyi-puteshestvennik-pereplyl-kaspiiskoe-more-iz-aktau-v-baku); [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/)
- **Conditions (conflicting):** ExplorersWeb reports "dangerously rough seas and high winds", with the boats rocking violently. In Baku, Bushby said, "We were lucky that the weather was wonderful. Nothing prevented us from swimming." Maxwell said the challenge was "more the mentality" than physical. — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/); [AzerTAc](https://azertag.az/ru/xeber/vsemirno_izvestnyi_puteshestvennik_nam_povezlo_s_pogodoi-3184406)
- Bushby said that failing at this "Plan B" "would be crippling". — [ExplorersWeb](https://explorersweb.com/karl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell-swim-across-the-caspian-sea/)
- It was billed as the first-ever swim across the world's largest enclosed inland body of water (a pre-swim claim). — [Times of Central Asia](https://timesca.com/caspian-sea-swim-by-travelers-carl-bushby-and-angela-maxwell/) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- The figures "31 days and 132 hours" and "two 3-hour sessions a day" are roughly consistent: 132 hours divided by 6 hours a day gives about 22 full swimming days, close to 27 swimming days with some short days.
- Maxwell's role after the swim is unclear. ExplorersWeb said it was unclear whether she would continue with him, and later sources describe Bushby walking Turkey alone.

### Gaps
- I found no source for the exact start date or the exact Kazakh launch point (Aktau town or a beach nearby).
- I found no official description of the rule for resuming each stage at the same logged position.
- The relationship between Bushby and Maxwell is not described in any fetched source.

## 5. Funding, health and personal life, 2006 to 2025

### Takeaway
Sponsorship collapsed in 2008, and new sponsors came in 2010. For about the last 16 years (from roughly 2010) his main backer has been a production company founded by Beau Willimon and Jordan Tappis, which is also backing a Werner Herzog documentary. His personal life was marked by long separations: an early marriage that ended in divorce, a son, Adam, who grew up largely without him, and two long relationships that ended.
