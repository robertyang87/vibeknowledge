# #18 — The three Schengen-reset flights to Mexico (2025–2026)

Research date: 6 November 2026. Format per item: link / date / short excerpt or paraphrase / licensing status. Items marked **[INFERENCE]** are my reasoning from the 90/180 rule and the surrounding anchors, not sourced statements.

**Disambiguation note:** Bushby also waited in Mexico from 2 May to ~19 August 2025, but that wait was for a *Turkish* visa (Turkey's 90-day rule, after his Bosphorus Bridge crossing), not Schengen — ExplorersWeb, 20 Aug 2025: Turkish restrictions "forced Bushby to leave the country the day he crossed the bridge… For the next three months, he waited in Mexico to be eligible to reapply for entry into Turkey." The three resets below are the Schengen ones.

  **Excerpt (BBC, as quoted by the tracker):** "Before Christmas [Bushby] reached the town of Gyor in Hungary – a few days' walk from the Austrian border… Speaking from Mexico, where he retreats every few months in order to satisfy visa requirements, Bushby said he would resume his walk close to the Hungary-Austria border in March…."

  **Licensing:** © BBC. All rights reserved; short quotation for reporting only. Tracker page © On The Go Solo.

- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby (Europe section)

  **Licensing:** Grokipedia content; treat as unverified synthesis.

**[INFERENCE]** A December exit fits exhausting a 90-day Schengen allowance begun in September 2025; the exact day cannot be fixed from open sources, and published day-counts conflict (an entry on 1 Sep would expire 29 Nov, yet multiple reports place him in Hungary into December), so no single exit date should be stated on screen.
