## #6(i) — The shotgun at his nose (Darién approach)

- **Book page (US edition):** ~p.135, inside Chapter 5, "Darien Gap" (pp.131–159).
- **2012 e-edition locator:** a "shotgun" search returns an anchor at PT211 —
  but that late position most likely reflects a later mention (he carried
  firearms against bears in Alaska), **not** the Darién scene. Do not use
  PT211 to locate this passage.
- **Status: UNRETRIEVED (wording).**
- **Close paraphrase (corpus-established frame only):** The passage sits in
  the Darién approach march of January–February 2001, the most dangerous
  walking of the whole expedition: after leaving Medellín on Mon 15 Jan 2001
  he moved through Dabeiba (21 Jan), Mutatá (Tue 23 Jan) and Bajirá (28 Jan) —
  a cross-check chain the report marks verified — travelling through
  guerrilla/cartel country where, by his own account in interviews, he
  disguised himself as a vagrant and treated the crossing as a military
  operation. The shotgun confrontation belongs to this stretch, days before
  his first detention at Boca de Cupé on Fri 16 Feb 2001. The corpus does not
  record the scene's own sentences; nothing further is asserted here.
- **Verbatim anchor:** none retrieved. Do not quote.

## #6(ii) — Boca de Cupé / the jail sequence

- **Book pages (US edition):** pp.159–166 (detention begins p.159; La Palma
  prison described p.164; the Metetí jail described p.166).
- **2012 e-edition locators:** "Meteti" → PT107–PT109; "prison" → PT106–PT108 —
  a tight cluster that corroborates the detention sequence's position in the
  e-edition.
- **Status: UNRETRIEVED (wording); content well established by the corpus.**
- **Close paraphrase (corpus-established):** He was first detained on
  **Fri 16 Feb 2001 at Boca de Cupé** (p.159), the river settlement where the
  patrol intercepted him. On **Tue 20 Feb** a patrol boat carried him onward —
  the only leg of the entire walk where a vehicle moved him forward, and the
  hinge of the episode's rule argument. The diary headings then run through
  Metetí (21, 22, 23, 28 Feb, 5, 10 Mar 2001). He was held across **three
  facilities** — Boca de Cupé, the La Palma prison (about 100 inmates,
  p.164) and the Metetí jail (described p.166) — for **≈18 days in total**:
  the often-retold "18 days" figure is right, the single-location Metetí
  version of it is wrong. Release is inferred as **Tue 6 Mar 2001** (three
  converging lines; 1 Mar not 100% excluded).
- **Verbatim anchor:** none retrieved. Do not quote.

## #6(iii) — Going back south to re-walk the boat segment

- **Book page (US edition):** ~p.166, at the end of the detention sequence.
- **Status: UNRETRIEVED (wording); the act itself is corpus-established.**
- **Close paraphrase (corpus-established):** After his release he did not
  simply continue north from where the authorities let him go. He travelled
  back south to Boca de Cupé — the point where the patrol boat had taken him
  aboard — and walked the boat-borne segment again on foot, so that the path
  remained unbroken. In the report's rules analysis this is filed as the
  founding precedent of his whole method: returning to the point and
  re-walking does not advance the route; every later vehicle use (visa runs,
  deportation flight, Schengen resets) follows the same leave-and-resume
  pattern set here.
- **Verbatim anchor:** none retrieved. The narration's anchor line for this
  beat must be our own paraphrase, not a presented quotation.
