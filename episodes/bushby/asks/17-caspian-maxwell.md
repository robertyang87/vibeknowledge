## 17(i) When and where Angela Maxwell joined the expedition

**Answer (best-supported synthesis):** Maxwell *re-joined* Bushby at **Bukhara, Uzbekistan**, for the 1,000-mile Kyzylkum walk to the Caspian coast — the pair flew to Bukhara together and set off on **24 September 2023** (his departure date, corpus-verified). Her own site, however, loosely dates the joint expedition to **2024** (the year they reached the coast and swam). The two had first teamed up in **Mongolia in 2017**, when Bushby invited her onto the "Steppes to the West" camel expedition — so the Caspian leg was a re-joining, not a first meeting.

- **Link:** https://www.shewalkstheearth.com/ (her own site, homepage)
  **Date:** accessed 6 Nov 2026 (page undated)
  **Excerpt (her own words, verbatim):** "In 2024, Angela joined her friend and colleague, Karl Bushby, for a 1,000 mile expedition walking from Buhkara, Uzbekistan through the Kyzlkum desert, and finishing on the coast of the Caspian, near the border of Turkmenistan. From there, along with two professional Azerbaijan swimmers, they set a world-record becoming the first people to swim across the Caspian Sea."

## 17(iii) The exact Caspian swim start point

**Answer: Aktau, Kazakhstan** — more precisely, the coast near Aktau (Mangystau Region), where the team was based and trained. The swim started **16 August 2024** (scheduled start, reported on the eve) and finished in Azerbaijan in mid-September 2024 (Turkish press reported completion on 18 Sep 2024), consistent with the verified 31 days / 288 km.

  **Date:** 8 August 2024
  **Excerpt:** "The expedition covering approximately 300 kilometers, from Aktau, Kazakhstan, to Baku, Azerbaijan, will begin mid-August and take about a month to complete."
  **Licensing:** © The Times of Central Asia. All rights reserved; short quotation for reporting only.

**Caveat:** no source found names a more specific point (beach, cape or port) than "the coast near Aktau." Note a loose end: Maxwell's site says the preceding walk finished "on the coast of the Caspian, near the border of Turkmenistan," while all swim-start reporting names Aktau, several hundred km up the Mangystau coast. The press consensus for the *swim start* is Aktau; her phrase is best treated as a loose description of the walk's landfall region, not the swim's start point.
