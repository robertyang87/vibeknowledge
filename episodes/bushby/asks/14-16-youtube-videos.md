**Corroboration that a Camels series existed on YouTube** — OnTheGoSolo's tracker page (social-media section, written September 2019; https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/): "Bushby's YouTube channel has only 3 videos on it and only 2 are about him. However, a search for the name 'Karl Bushby' brings up a longer list of videos. These include the four-part series about the Goliath Expedition, **the first part of the Camels series**, and a number of interviews and short news items… The most recent video, the recap of 2018, dates from January 2019." Two implications: (1) at least one Camels video was publicly findable via a YouTube name search in 2019; (2) it apparently sat among name-search results rather than (only) on his own 3-video channel — i.e. plausibly on an expedition/team channel. That channel could not be identified: no search result in this run returned a channel page for him or for the expedition, and YouTube watch pages fetched through our route expose only title/description/keywords — no channel name, upload date, or duration.

**Licensing note for STTW footage** — Angela Maxwell's expedition recruitment page (shewalkstheearth.com, "Open Seat Candidates", https://www.shewalkstheearth.com/open-seat-candidates.html) states the trek "will be a filmed expedition" and that every participant had "to sign a release form for Westward productions to use footage taken while on the expedition. Westward Productions is Karl Bushby's Sponsor," and that Westward's only expedition funding was the camels, their equipment and food. STTW footage rights therefore most plausibly sit with **Westward Productions** → APPLICATION REQUIRED via Westward (https://westward-productions.com/) and/or the expedition's own "Interviews and rights — Press and rights" route at karlbushby.world (per item 04). No self-serve licence exists; unlocated + unlicensed = not on screen.

**Context the checklist may want to correct** — the documented 2020 storm event of his Melaque period is **Tropical Storm Hernan (26–28 Aug 2020)**, not an "El Niño storm": ExplorersWeb, "Where in the World is Karl Bushby?" (2021-05-05, https://explorersweb.com/where-in-the-world-is-karl-bushby/) records that he "recently helped with rescue efforts after tropical storm Hernan flooded towns in southwest Mexico." No retrieved source uses an "El Niño" label for his 2020 storm experience; the checklist's wording may conflate the two. Hurricane Nora itself is documented (Category 1 landfall in Jalisco near Melaque/Barra de Navidad, 28 Aug 2021), and his base in Melaque during those years is multiply attested (Men's Journal; the 2015 Jordan Tappis interview, motionpictures.org) — but no source retrieved mentions him filming or uploading either storm.
