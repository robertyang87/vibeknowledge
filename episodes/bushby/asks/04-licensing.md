## Bottom line for the episode

- The only **self-serve** routes are the three agency platforms (AP Images, Reuters Connect, Getty) — all editorial, rights-managed/transactional.
- Bushby's own material has **two human doors, no storefront**: Westward Productions (documentary + his footage archive) and his own site's "Press and rights" route at karlbushby.world. Both are application/enquiry only.
- No public business email exists on his Instagram; the YouTube business email is behind a sign-in wall (not retrieved, per the no-login rule).
- The Touareg blog photos are effectively **frozen**: holder identifiable (Challenge4 GmbH / its credited cameramen) but the blog itself is down (HTTP 500); archived copies confer no rights.
