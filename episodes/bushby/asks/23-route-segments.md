## (b) Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan — two different lines, do not merge them

**Label: PARTIALLY SOURCED (both lines).** The asks wording blends two separate traversals four years apart. They are separated here because the sourcing differs completely.

### Line 1 — 2018–19: Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan border

**Sourced path (towns in order):** (Xinjiang, escorted to the Kazakh border) → **enters Eastern Kazakhstan, c. 2019** (his own words, above) → **Almaty** → (crosses into Uzbekistan **early June 2019**) → **Samarkand** → **Bukhara** → Turkmenistan border, crossed alone; at the Turkmenistan–Iran border by 15 Sep 2019 (first-person, asks/13).

- **Link:** https://karlbushby.world/story (official site, "The Silk Road" chapter)
  **Date:** accessed 6 Nov 2026
  **Paraphrase:** "Across the Kazakh border, Karl met up with Rika in **Almaty** before pressing west. The trail led through Uzbekistan and toward **Samarkand**… Together, Karl and Rika followed the Silk Road through Samarkand and the old trading hub of **Bukhara**, heading toward Turkmenistan." Then: severe heatstroke, hospitalised several days (Bukhara, summer 2019); "At the Turkmenistan border, visa restrictions forced a separation: Karl crossed alone."
  **Licensing:** © Karl Bushby / Westward. Paraphrase for reporting only.

- **Link:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Bushby
  **Date:** accessed 6 Nov 2026
  **Paraphrase:** He crossed into Uzbekistan in **early June 2019**. No Uzbek entry point or Kazakh intermediate town is named.
  **Licensing:** CC BY-SA.

- Father Keith's expedition update (posted 27 Aug 2019, relayed by the OnTheGoSolo tracker, https://onthegosolo.com/where-is-karl-bushby-2019/): Karl was at the Turkmenistan–Iran border "after walking through Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan" — country-level confirmation only. © OnTheGoSolo / expedition; paraphrase only.

## Method note

Sources were worked in this order: (1) local IG corpus dumps (`bushby_ig_caspian*.json`, `bushby_ig_latest.json`, `bushby_ig_turkey2025.json`, `bushby_ig_europe2025.json`, `bushby_ig_2017_mongolia.json` — the last is empty: the connector index does not reach 2017); (2) the official site; (3) trackers (OnTheGoSolo, ExplorersWeb); (4) press in English, German and Chinese. Nothing in this file comes from behind a login or verification wall.
