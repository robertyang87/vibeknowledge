## Verdict — documented negative, with one usable retrospective summary

The blog does **not** contain a day-by-day diary of the 17–31 March 2006 crossing, and it does **not** contain Kieffer's or Bushby's own account of the 31 Mar – 1 Apr 2006 landing/detention. The blog was not yet posting in early 2006:

- Monthly archives (mobile view, `?m=1`) for **February, March, April and May 2006 all return "No posts"**.
- The Atom/RSS feed queried with a date window (`feeds/posts/default?published-min=2006-03-01&published-max=2006-05-31`) returns **0 entries** for Mar–May 2006. (The feed itself is reachable, but the text extractor collapses feed XML to header lines, so the feed is not a readable route for entry text on any date; the zero-entry count for the window is nonetheless explicit.)
- The earliest posting confirmed in this run is **May 2007** — first-person field updates from Kieffer (e.g. his 16 May 2007 Egvekinot arrival post). So any 2006 material on the blog is necessarily retrospective.

What the blog does offer is (a) Kieffer's own retrospective section summary of the crossing, (b) a reposted December 2005 newspaper preview, and (c) outbound links to the contemporary press coverage of the detention — the blog hosts no detention narrative of its own.

## Landing / detention (31 Mar – 1 Apr 2006)

**No verbatim passage exists on this blog** — there is no first-person detention text by Kieffer or Bushby to quote. Blog internal searches for `detained`, `deported` and `uelen` return no 2006 detention post (`detained` → a 2012 Kyrgyzstan/Tajik-border post; `deported` → a 2011 Kamchatka post mentioning gulag deportations).

Paraphrase of what the blog does say/do:

- Kieffer's retrospective profile text dates the landing loosely — it says he reached/landed at **Uelen "in April 2006"**, while his own section table dates the crossing **17–31 March 2006**. Internal inconsistency; the section dates are the more precise of the two.
- For the detention itself, the blog's retrospective post links out to contemporary press coverage rather than narrating it: a **Seattle Times** archive item on the crossing/detention, and a **Boston.com (AP) item dated 2006-04-04** whose headline reports two men detained for crossing the Bering on foot, plus a New York Times link. (These are outbound links from https://nexusexpeditions.blogspot.com/search?q=uelen, accessed 2026-11-06; the linked articles are press items, not blog text, and are likewise not licensed.)

Short verbatim excerpt — the only decisive lines in Kieffer's own words on this blog (his retrospective section entry, 15 words):

> "March 17-31 2006 — 14 days, 5 days where swimming was required — 200 miles / 322 kilometers"

Paraphrase of the surrounding entry: the leg was completed by trekking, back-country skiing and swimming, hopping between ice surges/floes, among spotted seals and polar bears.

## Helicopter lift / drift episode — no blog corroboration found

- Blog search `helicopter` returns only a **May 2010 Paren, Kamchatka** post — nothing about the 2006 staging, the helicopter rescue off drifting ice, or Kieffer's frostbitten finger. The blog therefore provides **no dating of its own** for the pre-crossing helicopter lift; the corpus's adjudication (lift happened BEFORE the crossing, during hauling/staging; Seattle Times 2006-04-06) stands uncorroborated and uncontradicted by this blog.
- Blog search `Woolley` returns **zero posts** — no mention of the diary's Cape Woolley drift point anywhere on the blog.
- Caution: the reposted Dec 2005 News-Miner article does describe a drift-and-airlift — but it is about a **different team's earlier attempt** (Dansercoer/Henkels: airlifted after 8 days, drifted ~55 mi south), cited as precedent. It must not be mistaken for the Bushby/Kieffer 2006 episode.

## Discrepancies flagged for the FACTS card

1. **Distance:** Kieffer's own figure for the crossing is **200 mi / 322 km** — materially larger than the verified corpus's ~150 mi zigzag for the 58 mi strait. If the script uses a crossing distance, the two figures conflict and Kieffer's is a self-reported retrospective round number; do not silently merge them.
2. **Direction wording:** the same retrospective post calls it the "First Eastbound crossing completed in modern times" in its year summary and the "first successful Westbound crossing" in the section text. Internally contradictory; neither phrase should be quoted into the script without adjudication.
3. **Landing date:** profile blurb "landed in Uelen in April 2006" vs section dates 17–31 Mar 2006 (see above).
