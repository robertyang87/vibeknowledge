# Spatial edition review

## Factual scope

The narration is unchanged from the reviewed first edition. Primary claims
remain mapped in FACTS.md. The teaching position uses the checked legal move
sequence; A, B and C are empty legal candidate intersections. Illustrative
probabilities and readouts are synthetic, not KataGo measurements.

The three candidate proportions are a selected, normalized teaching subset;
other legal moves and passing are omitted. The network lattice is conceptual,
not a literal claim about the number or arrangement of KataGo's network
layers. The search tree shows alternating turns, with gold/white node roles
explained in the film. Evaluation follows the selected route back toward the
root. Ownership colors indicate hypothetical predictions, not secured land.
The comparison values use Black's perspective. A 90% forecast is explicitly
not a promise of nine wins in every ten games. Research on adversarial play
is qualified by model/settings scope, not asserted as a universal failure.

## Picture checks

All 22 scenes and the cover were rendered from the actual spatial composition.
A full contact sheet and selected full-size frames were inspected. Fixes
include enlarging the teaching diagram, separating black/white states,
reducing simultaneous bright network pathways, keeping training labels away
from routes, correcting reverse route order and distinguishing prediction
from result. The selection explanation treats prior and visits as exploration
inputs rather than an independent extra additive term. The camera uses actual perspective and XYZ positions. The glTF
asset is original Blender geometry, not a rendered photograph or a flattened
image masquerading as a moving spatial scene.

The visual grammar follows the supplied reference. Lemo's MIT gold-line
engine remains limited to its documented role; this is not represented as
literal Gilded Illumination or unmodified Art Deco. No demo sign-off appears.

## Sound checks

The exact user-selected repository track, We Never (Remix), is used. A
3.2-second equal-power phrase edit extends it to the narration length. The
75 BPM editing grid is an estimate, not a published tempo claim. Speech
compression, held ducking and level control preserve the narration.

The revised event table contains 97 timestamped effects: filament entries,
camera transitions, candidate/node contacts, search travel and returns,
training feedback and selected-path highlights. The master measured
-14.08 LUFS integrated and -1.56 dB true peak before video encoding.

Automatic recognition on the revised final mix used multilingual small with
medium rechecks of flagged scenes. All 22 scenes passed the same 0.92
Chinese/number comparison. Evidence: copy/mix-speech-check.json. This is
machine transcription validation, not a claim of human listening approval.

## Finishing and delivery

The in-progress production take uses a frame-exact 402-frame selection pickup
at frames 2232-2633 inclusive. It corrects the exploration explanation while
preserving scene timing. The final finishing pass also labels the reduced
9-line teaching diagram. Fresh source builds contain both corrected overlays
directly; finish_edit.py records the production pickup assembly.

The original AAC mix is copied, not retimed. The delivery report records final
dimensions, frame count, duration, full decode, archive CRC and post-AAC
loudness. Release assets are downloaded and checked against SHA256SUMS before
returning their links.
