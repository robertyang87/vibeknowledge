# KataGo — spatial gold edition

A Mandarin mechanism explainer in a dark, gold and ivory palette, following
the supplied reference's spatial light, particles and camera grammar. Use
abstract point/line diagrams with real depth and readable evidence.

## Three possible structures

1. Follow a move from network intuition to search and training feedback.
2. Contrast human intuition with the engine's calculations.
3. Start with the analysis dashboard and explain each number backwards.

Choose 1. Preserve the fact-checked 301.5-second Mandarin narration and its
22 scenes. The concrete educational thread is how limited computation can
produce strong moves; the ending returns to using AI as a research partner.

## Visual direction: supplied reference takes priority

The user supplied a 281.61-second, 640x360 reference video. Inspectable samples
are logged in research/VISUAL-REFERENCE.md. Its visual grammar is a sparse
black starfield, warm white/gold emissive filaments, traveling highlights,
particle convergence, depth/parallax, continuous diagram transformations,
serif Chinese captions, and camera pushes/pullbacks. Adopt a 1920x1080
landscape composition to follow the reference spatial grammar.

Use Lemo-Opuscar's actual Art Deco gline and goldGrad source (MIT) for engraved
metal line rendering, adapted toward the user's reference rather than forcing
stepped architectural frames. Reference instructions override those invariants:
no fixed UI chrome, no repeated card grid, no physical board, no ornamental
Art Deco title architecture, no period jazz. This is an explicitly documented
reference-led adaptation, not a claim of literal Art Deco fidelity.

Blender provides an original spatial golden lattice as editable mesh geometry
and a glTF export. Remotion/Three.js build the lifting search tree and animate
original point/line
chess-state diagrams, neural pathways, search branches, evidence revisions,
training feedback and camera framing. Gold means the selected or active path;
ivory means neutral structure. The subject stays readable through each move.
No reference footage, creator branding, claims or narration are reused.

## Sound and evidence

Reuse existing Mandarin narration. Use the user's We Never (Remix), Hi Noise
/ Qiu Cheng, with speech ducking and a phrase crossfade. Synthesized stone
contacts, electronic ticks, air sweeps and low impacts correspond to visible
actions. Diagrams and numeric values are pedagogical, not actual KataGo
analysis. Preserve factual qualifications and research notes.
